# AI & Advertising: 20 Meta ads

Twenty standalone video ads for **AI & Advertising by Ideabro Studio** (landing page: community.ideabrostudio.com/courses/ai-and-advertising).
Every ad has its own visual world and caption system, and they share the same offer and end card:
the IB mark, "AI & Advertising", $99 struck to $29, lifetime access and an "Enroll now" button.

- 14 vertical **9:16** ads (Reels, Stories) and 6 **4:5** ads (Feed).
- **5 in Hinglish** (A01, A05, A09, A10, A13). The rest are in English.
- 10 have voiceover (ElevenLabs **eleven_v4**). The other 10 are music and SFX with on-screen type. SFX use **eleven_text_to_sound_v3**.
- Captions sit between about 12% and 76% of the frame height, clear of the Reels/Stories UI.
- Masters are at −14 LUFS with a −1 dBTP ceiling, H.264 + AAC 256k, 30 fps.

Lessons from the competitor teardown (Piyush Glitch), applied here:
- We keep what works for them: kinetic type, word-synced captions, objections knocked out one by one, Hinglish variants, and the "the ad is the proof" approach (every frame of our product work was made with AI).
- We avoid their mistakes: no income claims, no made-up numbers, no other companies' logos in hero roles, and every keyword and CTA matches the landing page.

## Notes for launch

- **Name:** the ads say "AI & Advertising", matching the course page title. If you want "AI in Advertising" everywhere instead, it's one word in `core.js` (end card) plus a few lines of copy.
- **Price claims:** "$99 → $29, 70% off launch price" is taken from the page. If the launch offer ends, update the end card in `core.js` and every ad changes together.
- **A01 receipt amounts** are labelled "*typical estimate". They're illustrative, not a quote.
- **A12** shows a mock feed post with a fictional creator handle labelled "AI-generated creator". For Meta's AI disclosure, consider ticking the "AI-generated" label on the ad.
- **Sources:**
  - Ideabro Drive folder (AI films and UGC): only clean clips, no Shotless-branded exports and no IP lookalikes.
  - The course page stills, unbranded ones only.
  - Mixkit stock video and music (Mixkit free license).
  - ElevenLabs voices and SFX, plus the trailer score we generated earlier.
