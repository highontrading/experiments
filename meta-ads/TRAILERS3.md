# AI & Advertising: 20 ASMR-style trailers (S01–S20)

Twenty narrated Meta trailers, 9:16. All of them share one calm, premium motion language. Each one has its own colour world, frame shape and footage theme.

- **Motion:** every move is eased (sine / quint in-out), so nothing snaps. Footage drifts slowly inside a shaped frame. Shots change through organic, curved transitions: liquid blob, iris, wave, arch reveal, ribbon sweep, curved wipe, slow zoom and soft dissolve. Two shots crossfade inside the shape, so the trailer moves like one continuous canvas.
- **One visual, once:** no clip or still appears twice in the same trailer. Clips do repeat across different trailers. Each trailer keeps to one footage theme (drinks, beauty, Soho street style, UGC, wildlife, CGI billboards and so on).
- **Voiceover:** each trailer has its own narrator from ElevenLabs eleven_v4. Captions appear word by word in sync, each word rising softly out of a blur. S18 is Hinglish.
- **Sound:** ten new soft ElevenLabs music beds (silk, glass, velvet, air, satin, mist, warm, lux, dream, sitar) and ASMR-soft effects: glass chime, silk swish, soft whoosh, air swell, a soft click on the button and a shimmer on the end card. The music ducks under the voice, and every trailer is mastered to −14 LUFS.
- **CTA:** every trailer ends on a themed CTA: course title, ₹2,999 · one-time · lifetime access, an animated **Join now** button with a cursor tap, then the Ideabro end card.
- **Text check:** an automated pass compared every text box every 0.1 s, and all 20 trailers pass with no overlaps. I also checked stills from every trailer by eye. I swapped out shots that looked too similar inside one trailer, one clip with text burned into the footage, and clip ranges that started on a camera roll or a hard cut.
