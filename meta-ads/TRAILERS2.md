# AI & Advertising: 10 style trailers (R01–R10) + 10 Meta versions

Each trailer copies the **style** of one of the 10 reference videos you sent: layout, type pairing, text animation, transitions and pacing. Every one is about AI in advertising and built from your clips.

- **Length:** trailers run 40–47 s, all vertical 9:16, mastered at −14 LUFS.
- **Footage:** your new Drive folder (AI ads), your earlier Drive footage, course-page stills and a screen recording of your course page. Real stock footage (Mixkit) appears only as "the old way" (studio or crew shots).
- **No repeats:** no clip is used twice inside the same video.
- **Music:** 10 new ElevenLabs music tracks, chosen for punch (trap, phonk, drum & bass-style energy, epic trailer). Cuts land on the beat, and impact hits, risers, whooshes and bass drops sit on the transitions.
- **Voiceover:** four trailers are narrated with ElevenLabs eleven_v4: R02 (George), R04 (Adam), R05 (Liam), R07 (Brian). The rest tell the story through on-screen text, like their references.
- **Text check:** every trailer and Meta version went through an automated pass that compares every text box every 0.1 s and fails on any overlap. All 20 videos pass. I also reviewed stills from each one by eye and moved any caption that sat on a face or product.
- **Meta versions (`rNNm_…_meta.mp4`):** each one keeps the trailer's strongest opening section, then cuts to a CTA screen in the same theme: headline, 3 course bullets, ₹2,999 one-time, an animated **Join now** button and the course URL. It ends on the end card with a Join now button. Each runs about 22–33 s.
