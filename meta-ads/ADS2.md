# AI & Advertising: 20 more Meta ads (round 2)

Twenty new standalone ads for **AI & Advertising by Ideabro Studio**, each built on a theme not used in round 1.
Several draw on what the competitor (AI Video Bootcamp, ~840 live ads) does well: "This is AI" realism reels,
technique explainers with numbered prompt words, AI-or-real reveals, "how much does this ad cost?", and the
"AI can't do hands, right?" objection.

- **Price:** ₹2,999 is shown in **4 of 20** (B06, B07, B10, B17). The rest sell on the craft and leave price out.
- **Formats:** 15 × 9:16 (Reels/Stories) and 5 × 4:5 (Feed: B02, B04, B12, B15, B18).
- **Languages:** 5 in **Hinglish** (B07, B08, B09, B10, B11). The rest are in English.
- **Audio:** 11 have voiceover (ElevenLabs **eleven_v4**). All music is new, made with ElevenLabs **music_v2_5** (7 custom beds: ethereal, luxury house, epic, Indian pop, lo-fi, quiz, tension, synthwave). SFX come from **eleven_text_to_sound_v3**.
- **Captions:** each ad uses **one caption look from first frame to last** (font, colour, size, position), set inside the Reels safe zone.
- **Honesty:** "This is AI" / "Made with AI" labels appear only on AI-made visuals (Ideabro Drive and course-page work). Real stock footage is labelled REAL FOOTAGE (B09, B14, B16). No income claims, no made-up results, no third-party logos.
