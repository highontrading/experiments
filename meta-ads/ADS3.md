# AI & Advertising: round 3 (C01–C20)

Twenty more ads, each built on a **format** used nowhere in rounds 1–2. These are formats people already know how to watch: a movie trailer, a news bulletin, a search bar, a year-in-review, a dating app, a courtroom, a recipe card, a race, a museum, a comic, a horror teaser, postcards, cricket commentary, a whiteboard, a keynote, objection handling, a sung jingle, a meditation, a wedding invitation. The thinking behind it:

- **Pattern interrupt in the first second.** Every ad opens on a frame that doesn't look like an ad (a LIVE news bug, a search bar, a NOPE stamp, a horror flicker, a wedding garland).
- **One idea per ad.** Each ad lands one belief: the shoot is optional, AI looks real now, you can learn this, it's affordable, it's fun.
- **Proof on screen.** The product shown is the work itself: "Made with AI" labels sit on AI visuals, and real stock footage is labelled REAL.
- **Hooks for different buyers.** Founders (budget race, local brand), freelancers (proposal/courtroom), creators (jingle, Wrapped), sceptics (objections, search), Hindi-belt audiences (news, cricket, postcards, horror, jingle, wedding).

- **Formats:** 15 × 9:16 (Reels/Stories) and 5 × 4:5 (Feed: C06, C07, C10, C11, C15).
- **Languages:** 6 in **Hinglish** (C02, C12, C13, C14, C18, and the Devanagari/English mix of C20). The rest are in English.
- **Audio:** 11 VO ads (ElevenLabs **eleven_v4**; C17 uses two voices, a sceptic and an answerer). **C18 is a sung Hinglish jingle** with vocals, made with ElevenLabs music. 19 new music beds come from ElevenLabs **music_v2_5**, and 16 new SFX from **eleven_text_to_sound_v3**.
- **Price** appears in 4 of 20 (C02, C16, C17, C20), in the scene and on the end card. The rest leave price out.
- **Captions:** every ad has its own caption system, and it never changes mid-ad: kinetic one-word-per-beat (C08), karaoke lyrics with a bouncing ball (C18), news lower-thirds and ticker (C02), trailer title cards (C01), search typing (C03), Wrapped stat blocks (C04), swipe cards and stamps (C05), court-record transcript (C06), recipe handwriting (C07), race HUD (C09), museum placards (C10), comic bubbles (C11), glitching blood-red caps (C12), postcard handwriting (C13), cricket commentary ribbon (C14), whiteboard marker (C15), keynote gradients (C16), struck-through quotes plus lime answers (C17), soft serif (C19), gold invitation type (C20).
