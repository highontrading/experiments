# AI & Advertising: 5 trailers + 5 Meta ads

**Trailers (T01–T05)** are 27–33 s vertical films for Instagram Reels and the feed.
**Meta ads (M01–M05)** are 17 s cutdowns of each trailer. Each one has a hook, four proof shots and a CTA screen with a **Join now** button, ready to run.

**Rules for every video**
- **No repeated clips:** each source clip is used once per video. The trailers draw on 40+ clips across your new Drive folder and the older footage.
- **Vertical framing:** shots are cropped to 9:16 around the subject.
- **Captions:** one editorial system per video, modelled on the references you sent. Bold cream sans is paired with italic serif lines, with hand-drawn arrows and notes where it helps ("not a real person", "AI").
- **Placement:** captions sit in empty space, inside the Reels safe zone.
- **Audio:** music beds are ElevenLabs music_v2_5. Voiceovers are ElevenLabs eleven_v4 (T01 by Brian, T05 by Don). All videos measure −14 LUFS.
