Module 1 · ~30 min · Lessons 1.1–1.4

AI in advertising: the new playbook

Teach

AI doesn't replace taste. It replaces the shoot.

  • 1.1An ad goes through 6 steps: Brief → Concept → Visuals → Motion → Edit → Launch. AI makes visuals, motion and edits fast. You bring the idea and the direction.
  • 1.2The stack: Flow for video and images, ChatGPT for images, Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini for thinking, ElevenLabs for voice, Suno for music, Claude for editing, Meta Ads Manager to launch.
  • 1.3Start on free plans and pay only when you hit a limit. Set up one project folder and use the file naming from day 1.
  • 1.4The rules: use AI tools on a plan that allows commercial use. No celebrity faces, no competitor logos, no fake results. Check Meta's AI labels and the ASCI guidelines on launch day.
Show on screena quick tour of Flow and ChatGPT. Attach one skill in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini (30 seconds each).
Take careteach ideas, not menus (menus change). Don't quote tool prices; say "check the plan page".
Give studentsSTART-HERE, all 4 skills.
Assignmentpick your brand and take 5 product photos.

Module 2 · ~45 min · Lessons 2.1–2.4

Briefs, strategy and the big idea

Teach

A good AI ad starts with a good idea, not a good prompt.

  • 2.1A brief has 5 things: goal, audience, one message, must-haves, how we measure. Turn a messy WhatsApp message into a one-page brief.
  • 2.2Let AI read customer reviews and competitor ads (Meta Ad Library). Pick one angle: pain, desire, proof or festive.
  • 2.3The hook is the first 2 seconds. Types: visual shock, question, bold claim, pattern break, offer. Write 30 and keep 5.
  • 2.4A 15s script goes hook → problem → product → proof → CTA. The storyboard has 6 frames, and each frame becomes an image prompt.
Show on screenad-strategist taking a messy brief → clean brief → 30 hooks → 15s script → storyboard. Then fill in the Brand Bible.
Take caredon't accept AI's first idea; ask it to make the idea "less generic". One message per ad.
Give studentsad-strategist, Workflow 01, creative-brief, brand-bible.

Module 3 · ~50 min · Lessons 3.1–3.4

AI product photography

Teach

One phone photo can become a full studio shoot, as long as the label stays real.

  • 3.1Packshots: a good reference photo is taken in daylight on a plain background with the label sharp. Attach the photo and use lighting words (softbox, rim light, gradient background).
  • 3.2Lifestyle: put the product in scenes that fit the brand (vanity, Diwali, beach). Props tell the story.
  • 3.3Accuracy: zoom into the label every time. Fix it with one change per round in edit mode, regenerate, or paste the real label on in editing. Every image gets a verdict: SHIP, FIX or REGENERATE.
  • 3.4E-commerce set: white-background main image, infographic, scale shot, lifestyle shot.
Show on screenKesar photo → 4 packshots → 2 lifestyle shots → fix a warped label live → e-com set. Run Nano Banana 1.1 and GPT Image 2.6 side by side.
Take carealways attach the real photo. Zoom into the label on camera; that's the lesson.
Give studentsad-image-director, Workflow 02.

Module 4 · ~45 min · Lessons 4.1–4.4

Models, styling and virtual sets

Teach

Same face, same look, every shot. That's what makes it a brand.

  • 4.1Consistent model: make a character sheet (front, side, full body, expressions) and attach it every time.
  • 4.2Styling: describe fabric, colour and fit exactly. Keep the garment's real colours.
  • 4.3Indian skin tones and outfits: don't let AI lighten skin. Name the real outfit (Banarasi saree, Chikankari kurta) and a real setting.
  • 4.4Sets: mood board → one set → the same light in every shot.
Show on screenthe Kesar brand model sheet, a Loom Street lookbook (3 looks), and one set from 3 angles.
Take careno real people's faces. Check hands, jewellery and skin tone in every shot.
Give studentsad-image-director, Workflow 02.

Module 5 · ~60 min · Lessons 5.1–5.4

AI video ads and campaign films

Teach

Turn stills into ads that move. Each shot gets one move and one job.

  • 5.1Image to video: use your approved still as the start frame in Flow. Camera words: push-in, orbit, dolly, tilt. Make 2–3 takes and pick the best.
  • 5.2Multi-shot film: shot list → one keyframe per shot → one clip per shot. Keep the light, colour and wardrobe the same throughout.
  • 5.3CGI without a 3D team: giant product in a city, splash explosions, impossible scale.
  • 5.4Recipes: food pour, fabric movement, tech reveal.
Show on screenKesar hero still → 3 clips (push-in, orbit, reveal) with Google Omni Flash 1.2. Then a 4-shot 15s film and a giant serum bottle on the Mumbai skyline.
Take carenever wait on a render on camera. Always start product shots from the approved still, because text-to-video breaks labels. Keep clips 4–8s.
Give studentsad-video-director, Workflow 03.

Module 6 · ~45 min · Lessons 6.1–6.4

Sound, voice and music

Teach

Sound is half the ad. People feel it before they see it.

  • 6.1Voiceover: write for the ear in short lines. In ElevenLabs, pick a voice and direct the pace and emotion. Hindi in Devanagari script is pronounced better.
  • 6.2Jingle: a Suno brief needs genre, tempo, mood and the brand name in the hook. Also make a 5-second sonic logo.
  • 6.3Dialogue and lip-sync: use Flow's built-in audio for short lines, keep the lines short, and check the mouths.
  • 6.4Sound design: whoosh, pour, click, ASMR. Play the same ad silent, then with full sound.
Show on screenKesar voiceover in Hindi and English, a Diwali jingle, and the same 15s ad 3 ways (silent / music only / full sound).
Take carekeep the voice clear above the music. Check music rights on your plan.
Give studentsad-video-director, Workflow 03.

Module 7 · ~40 min · Lessons 7.1–7.3

AI UGC and brand ambassadors

Teach

UGC works because it feels real. Make it feel real, and stay honest.

  • 7.1AI creator: handheld camera, a real room, natural light, imperfect speech. Use one consistent creator.
  • 7.2Reviews, unboxings, testimonials: hook → first look → use → result → CTA. Write Hinglish scripts.
  • 7.3Brand face: an ambassador bible (look, personality, voice, wardrobe), so it's the same person every month.
Show on screencreate "Riya", the Kesar creator, then a 20s review and an unboxing.
Take carenever present an AI person as a real customer, and never fake before/after results. Mention The UGC Lab for going deeper.
Give studentsad-video-director, Workflow 03.

Module 8 · ~50 min · Lessons 8.1–8.4

Meta ads: statics, variations and testing

Teach

Meta rewards fresh creative. Make one idea into many ads and let the data choose.

  • 8.1Ratios: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1. Keep text out of the top and bottom safe zones.
  • 8.210 variations: change one thing at a time (hook, visual, offer or format).
  • 8.3Advantage+ creative: choose which automatic changes to allow, and protect the brand look.
  • 8.4Results: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA/ROAS. Keep the winners, kill the losers, remix the winners.
Show on screenone Kesar concept → 4 ratios → 10 variations → set up in Ads Manager (no spend). Paste a results table into ad-strategist and get the next round.
Take caredon't launch real spend on camera. Name files properly. Test one change at a time.
Give studentsad-image-director, ad-strategist, Workflow 04.

Module 9 · ~55 min · Lessons 9.1–9.4

Editing, end cards and brand kits (vibe editing)

Teach

You don't need a timeline. Describe the edit and direct it.

  • 9.1The 5 steps are Dump → Vibe → Beat → Layer → Sound. A vibe prompt has 5 parts: platform + length + energy + look + goal.
  • 9.2Big, readable captions (Hindi too) and one colour look for the whole ad.
  • 9.3End card: logo + one CTA + the offer, for 2–3 seconds.
  • 9.4Brand kit: save the colours, fonts, look, logo and sonic logo in the Brand Bible, so every edit matches.
Show on screenin Claude with vibe-editor, drop in the Kesar clips and type: "30s Reel, high energy, warm saffron look, bold captions, end on the product." Show the edit plan, give 3 director notes, add captions and an end card, then export 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1. Then show it in ChatGPT, and in Gemini (which gives a CapCut plan).
Take caretest ffmpeg before you record. Give at most 3 notes per round. Show the edit plan before it renders, so students see the AI think like an editor.
Give studentsvibe-editor, Workflow 04, brand-bible.

Module 10 · ~35 min · Lessons 10.1–10.3

Hybrid ads: real shoot + AI

Teach

The best ads mix real and AI. Shoot what must be real and generate the rest.

  • 10.1Shoot real hands, food texture and real people. Generate scale, locations and CGI.
  • 10.2Blending: match the light direction, colour and grain. Replace the background or extend the shot.
  • 10.3Phone upgrade: a 5-minute phone shoot turned into a premium ad.
Show on screena phone clip of Kesar in a hand → AI background and extension → blended 10s ad, with a before/after.
Take carematch the light; that's what gives it away. Keep the real product real.
Give studentsad-video-director, Workflow 03.

Module 11 · ~50 min · Lessons 11.1–11.3

Capstone: your full campaign

Teach

Put it all together: one brand, one campaign, start to finish. Then sell it.

  • 11.1Run Workflows 01 → 04 for the Kesar Diwali campaign, at speed.
  • 11.2Case study: problem → idea → process → work → results. Post it on Behance, Notion or Instagram.
  • 11.3Pricing and pitch: sell packages, not hours (starter statics, monthly creative, campaign film). Pitch with a free sample ad.
Show on screenthe full Kesar Diwali folder, the final ads, the case study and a rate card.
Take careprices are examples; tell students to adjust them for their market. Show the capstone rubric.
Give studentsall 4 skills, Workflows 01–04, capstone-brief.
