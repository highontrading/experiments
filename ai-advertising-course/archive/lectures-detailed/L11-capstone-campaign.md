- **Target raw length:** 55–70 min (3 lessons: 11.1 ≈ 25–30 min, 11.2 ≈ 12–15 min, 11.3 ≈ 15–20 min)
- **Lessons covered:** 11.1 Brief to launch: one complete campaign · 11.2 Turning it into a portfolio case study · 11.3 Pricing and pitching AI ad services
- **Level:** 3 (The agency standard)

- **Tabs open:** Claude (a fresh Kesar Diwali chat/Project), Google Flow (Google Omni Flash 1.2, Nano Banana 1.1), ChatGPT (GPT Image 2.6), ElevenLabs, Suno, Meta Ads Manager (test account, no publishing), Notion or Behance (for the case study).

- **The whole campaign must be pre-made**, because you'll run it fast and show finished outputs at each step:
  - The brief, angle, hooks H01–H05, the 15s/30s scripts and storyboard.
  - 6 statics in 3 ratios, the 30s film + 15s cutdown, 1 Riya UGC ad, 10 variations.
  - VO (English + Hinglish), jingle, sonic logo, 3 end cards.
  - The Ads Manager campaign built up to preview, plus a test plan.
  - The finished case study page (Notion/Behance) and a sample proposal PDF.
- A one-slide "campaign wall" (a grid of every asset) for the cold open.

## Lesson 11.1: Brief to launch, one complete campaign

Say **"LESSON 11.1"** on camera.

- **Goal:** Run the full pipeline (Brief → Concept → Visuals → Motion → Edit → Launch) on the Kesar & Co. Diwali campaign with `campaign-director`, so students see how every lecture connects.

**Talking points (say these as you move through each step)**
1. **"One skill coordinates all the others."** `campaign-director` walks you through all 6 steps, and at each step it uses the method of the matching skill.
2. Go through the capstone brief (`templates/capstone-brief.md`) and its deliverables: brief, brand kit, 6 statics in 3 ratios, 15s + 30s video ads, 1 UGC ad, 10 Meta variations, VO + jingle, end cards, test plan, case study.
3. **"Quality gates."** After each step, you stop and check against the 4 pillars before moving on: art direction, product accuracy, brand consistency, human touch.

**Live demo: the full run (fast; show pre-made outputs where generation would take time)**
1. **Attach** `skills/campaign-director/SKILL.md` and start:
   ```
   Use campaign-director. Brand: Kesar & Co. Saffron Glow Serum, ₹899, Diwali offer: free mini with every order. Brand bible v2 attached. Goal: sales on Meta in India, launch in 3 weeks. Run the full pipeline and stop after each step for my approval.
   ```
2. **Step 1, Brief** (≈3 min): it builds the one-page brief and asks the missing questions. Approve.
   - Gate: is the single-minded message ONE thing? → "Festive glow without the parlour."
3. **Step 2, Concept** (≈4 min): the angle, the big idea, hooks H01–H05, 15s/30s scripts, storyboard.
   - Show the big idea line on screen: **"Is Diwali, glow ghar pe."**
   - Gate: does every hook lead to the product?
4. **Step 3, Visuals** (≈5 min): it writes the image prompts (packshots, lifestyle, Meera, Riya). Paste one into Flow (Nano Banana 1.1) live; show the rest pre-made.
   - Gate: run `product-accuracy-checker` on the hero still. Verdict SHIP.
5. **Step 4, Motion** (≈5 min): the shot list + Flow prompts (Google Omni Flash 1.2), VO script with direction, a Suno brief.
   - Show the pre-made clips, VO and jingle.
   - Gate: is the label readable in every shot? Is the face consistent?
6. **Step 5, Edit** (≈4 min): it hands over to the vibe-editing method:
   ```
   Hand this to the vibe-editor method: 30s Reel, high energy hook, warm saffron look, bold captions, end on the product. Then a 15s cutdown and exports in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1.
   ```
   - Show the finished edits and end cards.
7. **Step 6, Launch** (≈4 min): the 10-variation matrix, file names, copy, Ads Manager setup checklist, `ad-policy-check` pass, and a test plan (what success looks like, when to judge, what round 2 will test).
   - Show the campaign in Ads Manager at the preview stage. **Don't publish on camera.**
8. Close with the campaign wall again. **"That's an agency's two-week job. You now know every step."**

**Show on screen:** a progress bar of the 6 steps at the top of the screen that fills as you go. A green "GATE PASSED" stamp after each check.

**Common mistakes**
1. Skipping the gates and finding a label error after launch.
2. Running the whole pipeline without the Brand Bible attached.
3. Trying to make everything perfect before launching anything. Launch, learn, iterate.

**Student gets:** `student-kit/skills/campaign-director/SKILL.md`, `student-kit/workflows/W10-brief-to-launch.md`, `student-kit/templates/capstone-brief.md`

**Assignment:** Your capstone. Run your brand through all 6 steps and deliver everything on the capstone list. Post it in the Capstone thread.

**Capstone rubric (say it out loud, and it's in `capstone-brief.md`):** strategy and idea 20 · art direction 20 · product accuracy 15 · brand consistency 15 · motion and sound 15 · Meta readiness 15. 70+ gets the certificate and a spot in the showcase.

## Lesson 11.2: Turning it into a portfolio case study

Say **"LESSON 11.2"** on camera.

- **Goal:** A one-page case study that gets clients and jobs.

**Talking points**
1. **"Clients don't buy images. They buy thinking they can trust."** A case study shows the thinking.
2. The structure (`templates/case-study.md`):
   - **The challenge:** brand, goal, constraint (budget, time).
   - **The insight and idea:** the angle, the big idea line.
   - **The process:** the 6 steps, with 1 visual each (show the prompts and the before/after: people love seeing the process).
   - **The work:** hero film, statics grid, UGC, variations.
   - **Results:** real numbers if you ran it. If it's a fictional brand, say so and show the test plan instead. **Never invent results.**
   - **Tools and time:** "Made in 3 days with Google Flow, ChatGPT, ElevenLabs, Suno and Claude."
3. Where to post: Behance, a Notion page, your site, LinkedIn as a carousel, Instagram as a reel breakdown.
4. Label concept work clearly: **"Concept campaign for a fictional brand"** or "Spec work for [brand], not commissioned".

**Live demo**
1. Attach `skills/case-study-and-pitch/SKILL.md`:
   ```
   Write a portfolio case study for my Kesar & Co. Diwali capstone. It's a fictional brand, so no fake results; show the test plan instead. Use the case-study template. Also give me a 6-slide LinkedIn carousel version and a 30s reel breakdown script.
   ```
2. Paste it into Notion. Drop in the hero film, the statics grid and 2 before/afters.
3. Show the pre-made finished page.

**Show on screen:** the case study page scrolling, the LinkedIn carousel slides.

**Common mistakes**
1. Only showing final images, with no thinking.
2. Fake numbers. One fake number destroys all your trust.
3. A 20-page case study. One page, scannable.

**Student gets:** `student-kit/templates/case-study.md`, `student-kit/skills/case-study-and-pitch/SKILL.md`

**Assignment:** Publish your capstone case study and share the link in the community.

## Lesson 11.3: Pricing and pitching AI ad services

Say **"LESSON 11.3"** on camera.

- **Goal:** Packages, prices and a pitch students can send this week.

**Talking points**
1. **"Don't sell 'AI'. Sell outcomes: more fresh creative, faster, for less than a shoot."**
2. Price the value and the deliverable, not your hours or your credits.
3. Four packages (in `templates/proposal-and-pricing.md`).

   **These are example ranges only. Your market, portfolio and city change them a lot. Start lower while you build case studies, and raise prices after every 2–3 happy clients.**

   | Package | What's in it | India (example) | Global (example) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **Starter statics pack** | 10 statics × 3 ratios, 1 revision round | ₹8,000–20,000 | $150–400 |
   | **Video ad pack** | 2 video ads (15s + 30s) + cutdowns + 3 ratios, VO + music | ₹20,000–50,000 | $400–1,200 |
   | **Monthly creative retainer** | ~20 statics + 6–8 videos + variations + a monthly test report | ₹40,000–1,50,000 / month | $1,000–3,500 / month |
   | **Campaign film** | 30–60s brand film, multi-shot, VO, jingle, cutdowns | ₹50,000–2,00,000 | $1,500–5,000 |

4. Your costs: tool subscriptions + credits + your time. Make sure every package has a healthy margin over credits. Use the calculator in the template.
5. Contract essentials: number of revisions, what counts as a revision, who owns the outputs, the client confirms rights to their product photos, AI use is disclosed, and the deliverables list.
6. **The pitch:** a free "spec ad" is your best door-opener. Make 3 statics for a brand you like, send them with a 3-line message, and offer the starter pack.
7. **"Your capstone is your first sales tool. Lead with it."**
8. Where to find clients: D2C brands on Instagram running ads (check Meta Ad Library to see who spends), Shopify stores, local businesses, agencies who need production help.

**Live demo**
1. `case-study-and-pitch`:
   ```
   I'm a freelancer in India. Build my 4 packages with example prices, a one-page proposal for a D2C skincare brand for the Video ad pack, and a 3-line cold DM + a short email pitch with my Kesar case study link.
   ```
2. Show the proposal and read the cold DM aloud. Example of what it should look like:
   ```
   Hi [Name], loved your new [product] launch. I made 3 Diwali ad concepts for it (attached), done with AI but directed like a shoot. If you like them, I can deliver a full Reels + statics pack in 5 days. Here's a similar campaign I did: [case study link].
   ```
3. Fill in `templates/proposal-and-pricing.md` with the calculator.

**Show on screen:** the package table, the proposal, the DM.

**Common mistakes**
1. Charging per image, so you race to the bottom.
2. Unlimited revisions.
3. Pitching "I use AI" instead of "I'll get you 10 fresh ads a week".

**Student gets:** `student-kit/templates/proposal-and-pricing.md`, `student-kit/skills/case-study-and-pitch/SKILL.md`

**Assignment:** Set your 4 package prices and send 3 spec-ad pitches this week.
