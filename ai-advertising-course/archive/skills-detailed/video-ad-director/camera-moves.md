# Camera moves for AI video ads (reference for video-ad-director)

Write the move in plain words: **speed + move + from → to**. One move per shot.

| Move | What it does | Best for | Prompt words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locked-off | Camera does not move | Label-critical shots, pours | "locked-off static camera" |
| Push-in (dolly in) | Moves toward subject | Reveal, premium, emotion | "slow push-in from medium to close-up" |
| Pull-out (dolly back) | Moves away | Reveal the setting / scale | "slow dolly back revealing the room" |
| Orbit / arc | Circles the subject | 3D product feel | "slow 90-degree orbit from left to front" |
| Truck / slide | Moves sideways | Product lineups, shelves | "smooth slide left to right past the bottles" |
| Macro slide | Tiny sideways move, very close | Texture, drops, fabric | "macro slide across the cream texture" |
| Crane up / down | Rises or lowers | Establishing, CGI scale | "crane up from street level to the top" |
| Top-down rise | Overhead, rising up | Flat lays, food tables | "top-down camera rising slowly" |
| Tilt up / down | Camera pivots vertically | Show height, reveal face | "slow tilt up from sandals to face" |
| Pan | Camera pivots horizontally | Follow action | "slow pan following her walk" |
| Whip pan | Very fast pan with blur | Energy transitions | "fast whip pan to the right" |
| Handheld | Natural shake | UGC, real-life feel | "handheld phone camera, slight natural shake" |
| FPV / fly-through | Flies through space | Surreal, CGI journeys | "FPV drone flies through the arch toward the product" |
| Rack focus | Focus shifts | From ingredient to product | "rack focus from saffron threads to the bottle" |
| Speed ramp | Slow-mo then fast | Splashes, action | "slow motion, then speeds up" (or do it in the edit) |

## Lens & framing words
- Extreme close-up / macro (texture, drops) · Close-up (product, face) · Medium (waist up) ·
  Wide (place, scale) · Low angle (power, scale) · Eye level (honest, UGC) · Top-down (flat lay)
- 24mm wide · 35mm natural · 50mm normal · 85mm portrait · 100mm macro

## Pacing guide
- Premium / beauty: slow moves, 5–8s shots, dissolves.
- Food / beverage: slow-mo hero + quick cuts around it.
- Fashion: medium pace, movement from fabric not camera.
- UGC: handheld, quick jump cuts (in the edit).
- Tech: slow reveals, sharp light sweeps, clean cuts.
