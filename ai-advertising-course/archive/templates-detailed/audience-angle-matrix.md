# Audience × Angle Matrix

**How to fill:** List 3–4 audience groups down the side and 4–5 angles across the top. Each cell is one
ad idea in one line. Then score each idea from 1 to 5 on **Pain** (how much they care), **Proof** (can we
show it), and **Fit** (does it match the brand). Make the top 3 ideas first.
Ask the `brief-and-strategy` skill to fill this from your brief, plus reviews and competitor ads you
found in the Meta Ad Library.

**Angle types to choose from:** Problem/Solution · Result · Ingredient/How it works · Comparison ·
Ritual/Routine · Gift/Occasion · Social proof · Price/Value · Identity ("for people like you") · Founder story

## Filled example: Kesar & Co.

| | Result | Ingredient | Gift/Occasion | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1: Working women 24–32, dull skin after long days | "Office-tired skin → Diwali glow in 14 days" | "Real Kashmiri saffron threads, not fragrance" | "Your Diwali party glow, sorted" | "Vitamin C stopped working? Try saffron." |
| A2: College students 19–23, first serum | "Your first serum, 3 drops a night" | "Saffron + niacinamide, simple and gentle" | "Glow for every Diwali photo" | "Same price as 2 coffees a month" |
| A3: Gift buyers 25–40 | "A gift that shows results" | "Saffron, the gift of royalty" | "The Diwali gift box she will actually use" | "Better than another candle set" |

| Idea | Pain | Proof | Fit | Total | Make? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 × Result | 5 | 4 | 5 | 14 | ✅ |

**Positioning:** For Indian women who want a natural glow without heavy makeup, Kesar & Co. is the
saffron serum that gives visible radiance in 14 days, because it uses real Kashmiri saffron with niacinamide.
