# Variation Matrix (1 idea → 10+ ads)

**How to fill:** Keep the big idea the same. Change only one or two things per variation, so when one ad
wins you know why. Fill the 4 lists (hooks, visuals, offers, formats), then build the matrix.
The `meta-ad-variations` skill builds this from your concept and names every file.

Naming rule: `BRAND_CAMPAIGN_CONCEPT_HOOK_FORMAT_RATIO_v01`
