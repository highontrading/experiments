# Proposal + Pricing

**How to fill:** Use the packages to price your AI ad services, then fill the proposal for each client.
**All prices below are EXAMPLES only**, to show the structure. Set your own prices from your cost, your
time, your city/market, your portfolio, and what the client saves compared with a real shoot.
The `case-study-and-pitch` skill can build packages and a proposal from your details.

---

## 1. Work out your price
```
Price = (your hours × your hourly rate) + tool costs + buffer for revisions (20–30%)
```
Then check it against what the client would pay for a real shoot (studio + photographer + models + edit).
Charging 30–60% of that is often an easy "yes" for the client and still good money for you.

## 3. Proposal template
**Proposal for:** [Client] · **Prepared by:** [You] · **Date:** [ ] · **Valid until:** [ ]

1. **What you told us** (their goal in their words, 2–3 lines)
2. **Our idea** (1 line + 1 sample image/mock)
3. **What you get** (deliverables table: asset, format, ratio, quantity)
4. **How we work** Brief → Concept → Visuals → Motion → Edit → Launch (with dates)
5. **Investment** (package + add-ons, payment terms: e.g. 50% upfront, 50% on delivery)
6. **Revisions** (number of rounds included; extra rounds charged)
7. **What we need from you** (product photos from 3 angles, logo, fonts, approvals in 48 hours)
8. **AI use and rights** (see clauses below)
9. **Next step** ("Reply YES and we'll send the invoice; we start within 2 days.")

## 4. Contract clauses to include
- **AI use:** "We use AI tools to create images, video, voice and music. Final assets are directed, checked and edited by us."
- **Product accuracy:** "We match your product's label, logo and shape to the reference photos you provide. You approve every final asset before use."
- **Rights:** "On full payment, you may use the final assets for advertising. Rights depend on the terms of the AI tools used; we use plans that allow commercial use."
- **Likeness:** "All AI people are made up. We do not copy real people, celebrities or influencers."
- **Claims:** "You are responsible for proving product claims you ask us to include."
- **Revisions:** "[2] revision rounds included. A revision is a change to an approved concept, not a new concept."
- **Payment:** "[50%] before work starts, the rest before final files are delivered."
