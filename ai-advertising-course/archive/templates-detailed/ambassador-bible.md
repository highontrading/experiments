# Ambassador Bible (your recurring AI brand face)

**How to fill:** Use this when one AI person appears in many ads: a brand model, a UGC creator or a brand
ambassador. Fill every line, then build the character sheet with `W02-consistent-model.md` and the
`model-and-set-director` skill. Paste the **CHARACTER BLOCK** into every prompt where this person appears.

Important: this must be a made-up person. Never copy a real person, a celebrity or an influencer.

## Filled example: Kesar & Co.

- **Name:** Ananya Rao, 27, Bengaluru. Product designer at a startup. Warm, honest, funny.
- **Why she uses it:** long screen days make her skin look dull; she wants a natural glow for photos.
- **Look:** oval face, dark brown almond eyes, natural thick eyebrows, small gold nose stud, warm medium-brown skin with real texture and a few faint freckles, shoulder-length wavy black hair, 5'4", slim.
- **Wardrobe:** everyday: oversized cream shirt, jeans. Festive: cream-and-red silk kurta, gold jhumkas.
- **Voice:** Indian English with natural Hinglish, relaxed, talks like texting a friend. Says "honestly", "okay so", "yaar". Never says "flawless", "fair", "guaranteed".
- **Settings:** her sunlit bedroom with plants, office desk, terrace at night with diyas.
- **AI disclosure:** "Ananya is an AI-created brand character." (in caption or as required by the platform)

**CHARACTER BLOCK:**
```
Ananya, 27-year-old Indian woman, warm medium-brown skin with real texture and faint freckles, oval face, dark brown almond eyes, natural thick eyebrows, small gold nose stud, shoulder-length wavy black hair, wearing an oversized cream shirt. Same person as the reference images, same face, same hair. Real skin texture, natural look, no beauty filter.
```
