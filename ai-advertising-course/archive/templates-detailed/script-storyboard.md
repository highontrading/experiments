# Script + Storyboard

**How to fill:** Pick the 15s or 30s structure. Write the voiceover and on-screen text line by line
with timings. Then fill the 6-frame storyboard. Every frame gets an image prompt (8-part formula)
and a video prompt (7-part formula), so you can go straight into Flow.
The `hook-and-script-writer` skill fills all of this from your brief and hook.

---

## 15-second structure
| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Hook | Stop the scroll: visual + text |
| 2–6 s | Problem or desire | Show the moment the viewer knows |
| 6–11 s | Product + proof | Product hero shot, one benefit, one proof |
| 11–13 s | Result | The feeling / the after |
| 13–15 s | CTA end card | Logo, offer, "Shop now" |

## 30-second structure
| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hook | Visual surprise + text |
| 3–8 s | Problem / world | Who this is for, what's wrong |
| 8–14 s | Product reveal | Hero shot, texture, label readable |
| 14–21 s | How it works / proof | Ingredient, demo, review |
| 21–26 s | Result / transformation | The after, the emotion |
| 26–30 s | CTA end card | Logo, offer, CTA, sonic logo |

## Filled example: Kesar & Co. "Diwali Glow" (15s, 9:16)

| Time | Visual | VO | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Macro of a saffron thread falling into golden serum | "Three drops." | THREE DROPS. | soft whoosh, sitar note |
| 2–6 s | Ananya at a mirror, tired, office bag dropped, diya lights behind | "Long days. Diwali in ten." | Diwali in 10 days? | ambient room, soft beat starts |
| 6–11 s | Dropper lifts from the Kesar & Co. bottle, serum on fingertips | "Real Kashmiri saffron, with niacinamide." | Real saffron + niacinamide | glass clink, beat |
| 11–13 s | Ananya at a Diwali party, skin glowing in warm light, laughing | "Your glow, ready." | Glow in 14 days* | crowd warmth, music lifts |
| 13–15 s | Bottle on cream silk with diyas, logo and CTA | "Kesar and Co. Shop now." | Shop the Diwali Glow · ₹899 | sonic logo |

**Frame 1 image prompt:**
```
Extreme macro of a single deep-red saffron thread falling into a pool of golden serum, tiny ripples, PRODUCT LOCK: the amber glass dropper bottle from the reference image stands softly out of focus behind, same cream label with saffron-red "KESAR & CO." wordmark, gold cap, do not redesign. Set: warm cream silk surface. Light: warm golden-hour side light, glowing highlights in the liquid. Camera: 100mm macro, f/2.8, very shallow depth of field. Mood: calm, luxurious. Brand look: palette saffron #E8862A, deep red #8E1B1B, cream #F6EBD9, gold #C9A24B. Format: 9:16 vertical. Avoid: misspelled label, clear glass, pump bottle, cold light, clutter.
```

**Frame 1 video prompt (Flow, Google Omni Flash 1.2, Frames to Video):**
```
Extreme macro shot. A single saffron thread drifts down and touches the golden serum, gentle ripples spread in slow motion. Camera: very slow push-in. Light: warm golden side light, liquid glows. Sound: soft airy whoosh, then one warm sitar note. Duration: 4 seconds, slow and calm. Consistency: same bottle and set as the start frame, label stays sharp and readable. Avoid: extra bottles, label changing, fast motion.
```
