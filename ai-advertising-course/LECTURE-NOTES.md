# Lecture Notes (short version)

One lecture per module. For each one: what to teach, the key points, what to show, what to
take care of, and what to give students.

**Demo product in every lecture:** Kesar & Co. Saffron Glow Serum (or any real product on your desk).
**Tools:** Google Flow (video: Google Omni Flash 1.2, images: Nano Banana 1.1) and ChatGPT (images: GPT Image 2.6).

## Before you record (once)
- 5 phone photos of the product (front, 2 sides, back, top) on a plain background in daylight.
- Flow, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, ElevenLabs, Suno and Meta Ads Manager signed in.
- Pre-generate the slow video clips. Start one render live, then show the one you made earlier.
- Record **in order**, because each lecture uses outputs from the one before.

## Module 1 · AI in advertising: the new playbook
**~30 min · Lessons 1.1–1.4**

**Teach:** AI doesn't replace taste. It replaces the shoot.

- **1.1** An ad goes through 6 steps: Brief → Concept → Visuals → Motion → Edit → Launch. AI makes visuals, motion and edits fast. You bring the idea and the direction.
- **1.2** The stack: Flow for video and images, ChatGPT for images, Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini for thinking, ElevenLabs for voice, Suno for music, Claude for editing, Meta Ads Manager to launch.
- **1.3** Start on free plans and pay only when you hit a limit. Set up one project folder and use the file naming from day 1.
- **1.4** The rules: use AI tools on a plan that allows commercial use. No celebrity faces, no competitor logos, no fake results. Check Meta's AI labels and the ASCI guidelines on launch day.

**Show:** a quick tour of Flow and ChatGPT. Attach one skill in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini (30 seconds each).
**Take care:** teach ideas, not menus (menus change). Don't quote tool prices; say "check the plan page".
**Give:** `START-HERE`, all 4 skills.
**Assignment:** pick your brand and take 5 product photos.

## Module 2 · Briefs, strategy and the big idea
**~45 min · Lessons 2.1–2.4**

**Teach:** A good AI ad starts with a good idea, not a good prompt.

- **2.1** A brief has 5 things: goal, audience, one message, must-haves, how we measure. Turn a messy WhatsApp message into a one-page brief.
- **2.2** Let AI read customer reviews and competitor ads (Meta Ad Library). Pick one angle: pain, desire, proof or festive.
- **2.3** The hook is the first 2 seconds. Types: visual shock, question, bold claim, pattern break, offer. Write 30 and keep 5.
- **2.4** A 15s script goes hook → problem → product → proof → CTA. The storyboard has 6 frames, and each frame becomes an image prompt.

**Show:** `ad-strategist` taking a messy brief → clean brief → 30 hooks → 15s script → storyboard. Then fill in the Brand Bible.
**Take care:** don't accept AI's first idea; ask it to make the idea "less generic". One message per ad.
**Give:** `ad-strategist`, Workflow 01, `creative-brief`, `brand-bible`.

## Module 3 · AI product photography
**~50 min · Lessons 3.1–3.4**

**Teach:** One phone photo can become a full studio shoot, as long as the label stays real.

- **3.1** Packshots: a good reference photo is taken in daylight on a plain background with the label sharp. Attach the photo and use lighting words (softbox, rim light, gradient background).
- **3.2** Lifestyle: put the product in scenes that fit the brand (vanity, Diwali, beach). Props tell the story.
- **3.3** Accuracy: zoom into the label every time. Fix it with one change per round in edit mode, regenerate, or paste the real label on in editing. Every image gets a verdict: SHIP, FIX or REGENERATE.
- **3.4** E-commerce set: white-background main image, infographic, scale shot, lifestyle shot.

**Show:** Kesar photo → 4 packshots → 2 lifestyle shots → fix a warped label live → e-com set. Run Nano Banana 1.1 and GPT Image 2.6 side by side.
**Take care:** always attach the real photo. Zoom into the label on camera; that's the lesson.
**Give:** `ad-image-director`, Workflow 02.

## Module 4 · Models, styling and virtual sets
**~45 min · Lessons 4.1–4.4**

**Teach:** Same face, same look, every shot. That's what makes it a brand.

- **4.1** Consistent model: make a character sheet (front, side, full body, expressions) and attach it every time.
- **4.2** Styling: describe fabric, colour and fit exactly. Keep the garment's real colours.
- **4.3** Indian skin tones and outfits: don't let AI lighten skin. Name the real outfit (Banarasi saree, Chikankari kurta) and a real setting.
- **4.4** Sets: mood board → one set → the same light in every shot.

**Show:** the Kesar brand model sheet, a Loom Street lookbook (3 looks), and one set from 3 angles.
**Take care:** no real people's faces. Check hands, jewellery and skin tone in every shot.
**Give:** `ad-image-director`, Workflow 02.

## Module 5 · AI video ads and campaign films
**~60 min · Lessons 5.1–5.4**

**Teach:** Turn stills into ads that move. Each shot gets one move and one job.

- **5.1** Image to video: use your approved still as the start frame in Flow. Camera words: push-in, orbit, dolly, tilt. Make 2–3 takes and pick the best.
- **5.2** Multi-shot film: shot list → one keyframe per shot → one clip per shot. Keep the light, colour and wardrobe the same throughout.
- **5.3** CGI without a 3D team: giant product in a city, splash explosions, impossible scale.
- **5.4** Recipes: food pour, fabric movement, tech reveal.

**Show:** Kesar hero still → 3 clips (push-in, orbit, reveal) with Google Omni Flash 1.2. Then a 4-shot 15s film and a giant serum bottle on the Mumbai skyline.
**Take care:** never wait on a render on camera. Always start product shots from the approved still, because text-to-video breaks labels. Keep clips 4–8s.
**Give:** `ad-video-director`, Workflow 03.

## Module 6 · Sound, voice and music
**~45 min · Lessons 6.1–6.4**

**Teach:** Sound is half the ad. People feel it before they see it.

- **6.1** Voiceover: write for the ear in short lines. In ElevenLabs, pick a voice and direct the pace and emotion. Hindi in Devanagari script is pronounced better.
- **6.2** Jingle: a Suno brief needs genre, tempo, mood and the brand name in the hook. Also make a 5-second sonic logo.
- **6.3** Dialogue and lip-sync: use Flow's built-in audio for short lines, keep the lines short, and check the mouths.
- **6.4** Sound design: whoosh, pour, click, ASMR. Play the same ad silent, then with full sound.

**Show:** Kesar voiceover in Hindi and English, a Diwali jingle, and the same 15s ad 3 ways (silent / music only / full sound).
**Take care:** keep the voice clear above the music. Check music rights on your plan.
**Give:** `ad-video-director`, Workflow 03.

## Module 7 · AI UGC and brand ambassadors
**~40 min · Lessons 7.1–7.3**

**Teach:** UGC works because it feels real. Make it feel real, and stay honest.

- **7.1** AI creator: handheld camera, a real room, natural light, imperfect speech. Use one consistent creator.
- **7.2** Reviews, unboxings, testimonials: hook → first look → use → result → CTA. Write Hinglish scripts.
- **7.3** Brand face: an ambassador bible (look, personality, voice, wardrobe), so it's the same person every month.

**Show:** create "Riya", the Kesar creator, then a 20s review and an unboxing.
**Take care:** never present an AI person as a real customer, and never fake before/after results. Mention The UGC Lab for going deeper.
**Give:** `ad-video-director`, Workflow 03.

## Module 8 · Meta ads: statics, variations and testing
**~50 min · Lessons 8.1–8.4**

**Teach:** Meta rewards fresh creative. Make one idea into many ads and let the data choose.

- **8.1** Ratios: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1. Keep text out of the top and bottom safe zones.
- **8.2** 10 variations: change one thing at a time (hook, visual, offer or format).
- **8.3** Advantage+ creative: choose which automatic changes to allow, and protect the brand look.
- **8.4** Results: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA/ROAS. Keep the winners, kill the losers, remix the winners.

**Show:** one Kesar concept → 4 ratios → 10 variations → set up in Ads Manager (no spend). Paste a results table into `ad-strategist` and get the next round.
**Take care:** don't launch real spend on camera. Name files properly. Test one change at a time.
**Give:** `ad-image-director`, `ad-strategist`, Workflow 04.

## Module 9 · Editing, end cards and brand kits (vibe editing)
**~55 min · Lessons 9.1–9.4**

**Teach:** You don't need a timeline. Describe the edit and direct it.

- **9.1** The 5 steps are Dump → Vibe → Beat → Layer → Sound. A vibe prompt has 5 parts: platform + length + energy + look + goal.
- **9.2** Big, readable captions (Hindi too) and one colour look for the whole ad.
- **9.3** End card: logo + one CTA + the offer, for 2–3 seconds.
- **9.4** Brand kit: save the colours, fonts, look, logo and sonic logo in the Brand Bible, so every edit matches.

**Show:** in Claude with `vibe-editor`, drop in the Kesar clips and type: *"30s Reel, high energy, warm saffron look, bold captions, end on the product."* Show the edit plan, give 3 director notes, add captions and an end card, then export 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1. Then show it in ChatGPT, and in Gemini (which gives a CapCut plan).
**Take care:** test ffmpeg before you record. Give at most 3 notes per round. Show the edit plan before it renders, so students see the AI think like an editor.
**Give:** `vibe-editor`, Workflow 04, `brand-bible`.

## Module 10 · Hybrid ads: real shoot + AI
**~35 min · Lessons 10.1–10.3**

**Teach:** The best ads mix real and AI. Shoot what must be real and generate the rest.

- **10.1** Shoot real hands, food texture and real people. Generate scale, locations and CGI.
- **10.2** Blending: match the light direction, colour and grain. Replace the background or extend the shot.
- **10.3** Phone upgrade: a 5-minute phone shoot turned into a premium ad.

**Show:** a phone clip of Kesar in a hand → AI background and extension → blended 10s ad, with a before/after.
**Take care:** match the light; that's what gives it away. Keep the real product real.
**Give:** `ad-video-director`, Workflow 03.

---

## Module 11 · Capstone: your full campaign
**~50 min · Lessons 11.1–11.3**

**Teach:** Put it all together: one brand, one campaign, start to finish. Then sell it.

- **11.1** Run Workflows 01 → 04 for the Kesar Diwali campaign, at speed.
- **11.2** Case study: problem → idea → process → work → results. Post it on Behance, Notion or Instagram.
- **11.3** Pricing and pitch: sell packages, not hours (starter statics, monthly creative, campaign film). Pitch with a free sample ad.

**Show:** the full Kesar Diwali folder, the final ads, the case study and a rate card.
**Take care:** prices are examples; tell students to adjust them for their market. Show the capstone rubric.
**Give:** all 4 skills, Workflows 01–04, `capstone-brief`.
