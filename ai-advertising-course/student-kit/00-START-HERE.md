# AI & Advertising: Student Kit

Welcome. This kit is your toolbox for the whole course. Every lesson tells you which file to
open. You don't need to read it all now.

## What's inside

| Folder | What it is | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| `skills/` | **17 skills**: instruction files that turn Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini into a specialist (creative director, product photographer, video director, editor…). They write your prompts for you. | Every lesson |
| `templates/` | Fill-in documents: brief, brand bible, hooks, scripts, shot list, variation matrix, test log, case study, pricing | Modules 1, 2, 8, 9, 11 |
| `workflows/` | **W01–W10**: step-by-step recipes (tool → action → skill → output → check) | Whenever you make something |
| `prompt-library/` | Ready-made prompts for images, video (Google Flow) and audio, plus the **Recipe Vault** (13 ad formats) | When you want a fast starting point |

## Your 5-minute setup

1. **Pick your brand.** Use your own, a client's (with permission), or a made-up one. You will
   use the same brand in every module, and by Module 11 it becomes your capstone campaign.
2. **Make a project folder:**
   ```
   MYBRAND_CAMPAIGN/
     01_brief  02_images  03_video  04_audio  05_edit  06_launch
   ```
3. **Take 5 product photos** on a plain background in daylight: front, 45° left, 45° right,
   back label, top. These are your "product lock" references. Keep the label sharp.
4. **Attach your first skill.** Read `HOW-TO-USE-SKILLS.md`, then attach
   `skills/campaign-director/` in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.
5. **Fill your Brand Bible.** Attach `skills/brand-bible-builder/` and let it interview you. Save
   the result as `01_brief/brand-bible.md`. You will attach this file again and again.

## Main tools

| Job | Tool | Default model |
|---|---|---|
| Video | Google Flow | Google Omni Flash 1.2 |
| Images | Google Flow / Gemini or ChatGPT | Nano Banana 1.1 or GPT Image 2.6 |
| Prompts, scripts, strategy | Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini + our skills | any |
| Voice | ElevenLabs | — |
| Music | Suno | — |
| Editing | Vibe editing with Claude (`vibe-editor` skill), or CapCut | — |
| Launch | Meta Ads Manager | — |

You can use other models too. The skills write prompts in a structure that works with any
good image or video model.

## The course in one line

**Brief → Concept → Visuals → Motion → Edit → Launch**, directed by you.
The tools are easy; taste is the skill. Check every output against the 4 pillars:
**art direction, product accuracy, brand consistency, human touch.**
