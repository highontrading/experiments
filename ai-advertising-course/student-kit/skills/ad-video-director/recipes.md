# Ad Video Director: Recipes

Ready-to-paste prompts for **Google Flow** with **Google Omni Flash 1.2**. Swap the words in
[brackets] for your own product. For start frames, use Nano Banana 1.1 or GPT Image 2.6.

**Formula:** `SHOT TYPE + SUBJECT & ACTION + CAMERA MOVE + LIGHT & MOOD + SOUND + DURATION/PACE + CONSISTENCY` + `Avoid:`

**Standard CONSISTENCY line** (it's in every prompt below):
```
Consistency: same product, set and light as the start frame. The label stays sharp and readable and the product does not change shape.
```

---

## 1. Camera moves

Write the move as **speed + move + from → to**, and use one move per shot.

| Move | What it does | Best for | Prompt words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locked-off | Camera does not move | Label shots, pours | "locked-off static camera" |
| Push-in | Moves toward the subject | Reveal, premium, emotion | "slow push-in from medium to close-up" |
| Pull-out | Moves away | Reveal the setting or scale | "slow pull-out revealing the room" |
| Orbit | Circles the subject | 3D product feel | "slow 90-degree orbit from left to front" |
| Slide | Moves sideways | Product lineups, parallax | "smooth slide left to right" |
| Macro slide | Tiny sideways move, very close | Texture, drops, fabric | "macro slide across the cream texture" |
| Crane up / down | Rises or lowers | Establishing shots, CGI scale | "crane up from street level to the top" |
| Top-down | Looks straight down | Flat lays, food tables | "top-down camera rising slowly" |
| Tilt up | Pivots up | Show height, reveal a face | "slow tilt up from the base to the cap" |
| Whip pan | Very fast pan with blur | Energy transitions | "fast whip pan to the right" |
| Handheld | Natural shake | UGC, real-life feel | "handheld phone camera, slight natural shake" |
| FPV fly-through | Flies through space | Surreal journeys | "FPV drone flies through the arch toward the product" |
| Rack focus | Focus shifts | Ingredient → product | "rack focus from saffron threads to the bottle" |

**Lenses:** macro / 100mm for texture · 85mm for portraits · 50mm normal · 35mm natural ·
24mm wide · "shot on a phone, 26mm" for a UGC look.
**Pacing:** beauty = slow 5–8s shots · food = slow-mo hero shot + quick cuts · fashion =
movement from the fabric · UGC = handheld + jump cuts · tech = slow reveals + light sweeps.

## 2. Product motion

**Slow push-in**
```
Close-up product shot. The [Kesar & Co. serum bottle] stands still on cream silk while tiny dust particles float in the light. Camera: very slow, smooth push-in towards the label. Light & mood: warm golden side light, calm and premium. Sound: soft room tone, one gentle sitar note. Duration: 5 seconds, slow. Consistency: same product, set and light as the start frame, label sharp and readable, product does not change shape. Avoid: fast motion, label changing, extra objects.
```

**Orbit on a plinth**
```
Medium close-up product shot. The [product] stands on a round travertine plinth. Camera: smooth 45-degree orbit from left to right at product height. Light & mood: hard golden window light, leaf shadows moving slightly. Sound: soft whoosh, light ambience. Duration: 6 seconds, smooth and steady. Consistency: same product, set and light as the start frame, label sharp and readable, product does not change shape. Avoid: full 360 spin, wobble, label warping.
```

**Dropper lift**
```
Close-up. A hand lifts the gold dropper out of the [product] bottle; one golden drop forms at the tip and falls. Camera: locked-off. Light & mood: warm backlight that makes the drop glow. Sound: soft glass clink, a tiny drip. Duration: 5 seconds, slow and precise. Consistency: same bottle and hand as the start frame, label readable, hand has five fingers. Avoid: extra fingers, the dropper changing shape, fast motion.
```

**Spin to label**
```
Close-up. The [product] slowly turns on a rotating stand and stops with the label facing the camera. Camera: static. Light & mood: clean studio light, premium. Sound: soft click at the stop. Duration: 5 seconds. Consistency: same product as the start frame, label text unchanged when it faces the camera. Avoid: fast spin, label text changing.
```

**Low-angle luxury tilt-up**
```
Low-angle hero shot. The [product] stands on a black glossy surface. Camera: slow tilt up from the reflection to the gold cap. Light & mood: dark luxury, narrow strip light, gold rim glow. Sound: deep low hum, soft shimmer. Duration: 5 seconds. Consistency: same product, set and light as the start frame, label sharp. Avoid: grey blacks, flicker.
```

## 3. Pours and splashes (food & drink)

**Serum pour**
```
Close-up. Golden serum pours slowly from above into a small glass dish, forming a smooth glossy ribbon. Camera: slow push-in. Light & mood: warm backlight, rich glow. Sound: soft liquid pour, gentle ambience. Duration: 5 seconds, slow motion. Consistency: same set and light as the start frame. Avoid: splashing, a watery or oily look.
```

**Drink splash (Fizzbar)**
```
Slow-motion product shot. The [Fizzbar can] bursts up through a wave of mango juice, droplets and mango slices flying past the camera. Camera: slight slow push-in. Light & mood: bright backlight, glowing droplets, fresh and energetic. Sound: big fizzy splash, can hiss, percussion hit. Duration: 4 seconds, slow motion. Consistency: same can as the start frame, logo readable at the end. Avoid: deformed can, brown juice, logo melting.
```

**Can open + fizz**
```
Close-up. A finger pulls the tab of the cold [Fizzbar can]; fine fizz mist escapes and condensation drops run down. Camera: static macro. Light & mood: cool rim light, warm front light. Sound: crisp can crack and fizz, very close. Duration: 4 seconds. Consistency: same can as the start frame, label readable. Avoid: extra fingers, the can changing shape.
```

**Pour into a glass**
```
Macro slow-motion. Ice-cold [Fizzbar] pours from the can into a tall glass with ice; bubbles rise and a mint leaf spins. Camera: locked-off, slight push-in. Light & mood: hard backlight through the drink, condensation sparkling, thirsty and fresh. Sound: can pop, pour, fizz, ice clink. Duration: 5 seconds, slow motion. Consistency: can label as in the start frame, can on the left. Avoid: liquid going the wrong way, label changes, splashes over the logo.
```

**Chai pour**
```
Medium close-up. Masala chai is poured from a steel kettle from high up into a glass, making a frothy top, steam rising. Camera: static, slightly low. Light & mood: warm morning kitchen light, cosy. Sound: chai pour, a spoon clinking, kitchen ambience. Duration: 5 seconds. Consistency: same set as the start frame. Avoid: liquid floating, too much steam.
```

Sound: soft fabric swish, a gentle music swell. Duration: 5 seconds, slow motion. Consistency: same woman and saree pattern as the start frame. Avoid: fabric merging with her body, the pattern melting.

**Fabric macro**

Extreme close-up. Hand-block-printed cotton moves gently as fingers run across it. Camera: slow macro slide left. Light & mood: soft side light showing the weave. Sound: soft fabric rustle. Duration: 4 seconds. Consistency: same print as the start frame. Avoid: a digital-looking pattern.

## 7. UGC handheld

Add this **UGC realism block** to any shot: `phone-camera look, auto exposure, slight handheld shake, natural skin texture, real home setting, imperfect framing, ambient room sound`.

**Selfie review**
```
Handheld selfie video on a phone front camera. [Creator, same as the ambassador references] sits on her bed, holds the [product] up to the camera and says in natural Hinglish: "Yaar, honestly… three drops at night, and look at my skin." Camera: handheld, small natural movements, slightly high angle. Light & mood: natural window light, real bedroom, casual. Sound: her voice close to the phone mic, quiet room tone, no music. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same woman and product as the start frame, label readable when she holds it up, lips in sync with the line. Avoid: studio look, perfectly stable camera, beauty filter, label morphing.
```

**Unboxing (POV hands)**
```
POV handheld unboxing. Hands open a cream Kesar & Co. box, peel back tissue paper and lift the [product] toward the camera. Camera: POV, handheld, natural. Light & mood: daylight on a desk. Sound: cardboard opening, paper crinkle, a happy "ooh". Duration: 7 seconds. Consistency: same box and product as the start frame, label readable at the end. Avoid: extra hands, the product appearing from nowhere.
```

**GRWM mirror**
```
Handheld phone video in a bathroom mirror. [Creator] puts three drops of the serum on her fingertips and pats it into her cheeks while smiling at the mirror. Camera: phone held in one hand, small shakes. Light & mood: bathroom light plus window light, real and casual. Sound: soft room echo, a light background beat. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same woman and product as the start frame. Avoid: perfect studio light, extra fingers, a changed face.
```

**Try-on haul**
```
Handheld mirror video. [Creator] steps into frame wearing the [Loom Street indigo kurta], turns once to show the flare, then pinches the fabric toward the camera and says: "True to size, and it's so soft." Camera: phone held at chest height, slight shake. Light & mood: bedroom daylight, casual. Sound: her voice, a soft fabric swish, room tone. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same woman, same kurta print and colour as the start frame. Avoid: print changing, stiff pose, studio look.
```

## 9. Voice (ElevenLabs)

**Voice descriptions**
```
Warm Indian English female voice, late 20s, calm and confident, soft smile in the voice, medium-slow pace, natural, not announcer-like.
```
```
Young Indian male voice, early 20s, energetic and friendly, Hinglish-friendly, fast but clear, like a creator talking to friends.
```
```
Deep cinematic male voice, Indian English, slow and dramatic, for a premium brand film.
```

**Settings:** use a multilingual model for Hindi. Stability medium (about 40–50%), similarity
high, style low to medium. Make 3 takes per line and keep the most natural.

**English VO · 15s**
```
Three drops.
Long days... Diwali in ten.
Real Kashmiri saffron, with niacinamide.
Your glow, READY.
Kay-sar and Co. Shop the Diwali Glow.
```

**Hindi VO · 15s** (Devanagari for better pronunciation; brand names in English letters)
```
बस तीन बूँदें।
लंबी थकी हुई शामें... और दिवाली सिर्फ़ दस दिन दूर।
असली कश्मीरी केसर, niacinamide के साथ।
आपका glow, तैयार।
Kesar and Co. Diwali Glow, अभी shop करें।
```

**Hinglish VO · 15s** (creator style)
```
POV: guests दस मिनट में आ रहे हैं…
…और skin थकी-थकी लग रही है।
बस एक drop… Kesar and Co. Saffron Glow.
Festive glow… सिर्फ़ ONE minute में।
Shop now.
```
If a Hinglish line sounds odd, try it fully in Roman letters ("Bas ek drop… Kesar and Co.") and compare.

**Delivery notes**
| Line | Direction |
|---|---|
| Hook | Slow, almost a whisper, like sharing a secret |
| Problem | Tired, relatable, a small sigh first |
| Product | Clear and proud, slower on the product name |
| CTA | Warm smile in the voice, upbeat, not shouting |

## 12. Hybrid: real footage + AI

**Restyle a real phone frame** (Nano Banana 1.1 / GPT Image 2.6)
```
SUBJECT: use the attached photo: a hand holding the serum bottle. Keep the hand, the bottle and their exact position.
PRODUCT LOCK: keep the exact Kesar & Co. Saffron Glow Serum: same label text, logo, gold cap, amber glass and shape. Do not redesign.
SET: replace the messy room with a warm marble vanity, a brass tray and marigold petals, softly out of focus.
LIGHT: keep the soft window light from the left as in the photo; add a gentle warm rim on the glass.
CAMERA: same angle and framing as the photo, phone lens look, 9:16.
STYLING & MOOD: premium, calm, festive.
BRAND LOOK: warm golden; saffron, deep red, cream, gold.
FORMAT: 9:16 photo.
Avoid: changing the hand, extra fingers, new label text, plastic skin, studio flash.
```

**Extend a real clip into an AI world** (Frames to Video from the last real frame, or Extend)
```
Shot type: close-up turning into a wide reveal, vertical 9:16.
Subject & action: starting from this exact frame (a hand holding the serum), the camera pulls back as the room dissolves into a golden saffron field at sunset; the hand and bottle stay sharp and centred.
Camera move: smooth pull-back with a slight rise.
Light & mood: warm sunset backlight, same left-side light as the start frame, dreamy.
Sound: soft wind, a rising shimmer.
Duration/pace: 5 seconds, slow and smooth.
Consistency: same hand, skin tone, bottle and label as the start frame; label stays readable.
Avoid: hand morphing, label changing, extra bottles, sudden cuts.
```
