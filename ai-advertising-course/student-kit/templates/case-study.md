# Portfolio Case Study

**How to fill:** Turn your capstone (or any client project) into a case study for Behance, Notion, your
website or a PDF for pitches. Clients buy your **thinking and process**, not only the final ad. Keep it
visual: one image or clip for every section. The `case-study-and-pitch` skill writes the first draft from
your notes, brief and results.

Structure: **Problem → Insight → Idea → Process → Work → Results → What I'd do next**

## Filled example (short)

**Kesar & Co.: Diwali Glow: a full saffron-glow campaign without a shoot**

- **Problem:** A new Indian skincare brand needed festive ads in 7 days, with no studio, models or budget for a shoot.
- **Insight:** Working women don't want "more skincare" before Diwali. They want to look rested in photos.
- **Idea:** "Three drops to Diwali glow": the saffron thread becomes the hero.
- **Process:** brief → 30 hooks → 5 picked → 6-frame storyboard → packshots from one phone photo → Ananya character sheet → 6 shots in Google Flow → Hindi + English VO → Suno jingle → vibe-edited in Claude → 10 variations.
- **Work:** 30s film, 2 cut-downs, 6 statics, 1 UGC review, 10 variations.
- **Results:** Self-initiated concept. Planned test: 4 hooks × 3 visuals; success = CPA under ₹450.
- **Next:** test a real customer review against the AI-character UGC.
