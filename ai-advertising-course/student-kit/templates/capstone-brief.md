# Capstone Brief: Your Full Campaign

**What you do:** Make one complete campaign for your own brand (real, a client's with permission, or
made up), from brief to launch-ready ads. Use the brand you picked in Lecture 1. If you did each module's
assignment, about 70% of this is already done.

Follow `workflows/W10-brief-to-launch.md`. Attach the `campaign-director` skill to run the whole flow.

## Rubric (100 points)
| Area | Points | Full marks looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy** | 20 | Clear audience and insight, sharp one-line idea, hooks that match the audience, test plan with a win rule |
| **Art direction** | 20 | Light, colour, composition and styling look expensive and intentional; no "AI look" (plastic skin, messy hands, random props) |
| **Product accuracy** | 15 | Label text, logo, shape and colour match the real product in every asset |
| **Brand consistency** | 15 | One look across all images and videos; same model, palette, type and end card |
| **Motion & sound** | 15 | Smooth, purposeful camera moves; clean cuts on the beat; clear VO; music and SFX that fit; good mix |
| **Meta readiness** | 15 | All ratios, safe zones respected, captions, end card + CTA, correct names, policy checklist done |
| **Total** | **100** | 70+ = certificate · 85+ = featured in the showcase |
