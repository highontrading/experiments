client) checks. The `campaign-director` skill can draft it from your brief.

## Filled example: Kesar & Co. "Diwali Glow"

- **Big idea:** Real saffron glow, ready before Diwali.
- **Objective:** Sales. KPI: CPA under ₹450, ROAS above 2.0.
- **Dates:** Production 7 days → launch D-21 → run until Diwali + 2 days.
- **Assets:** 1 x 30s film (9:16), 2 x 15s cut-downs (9:16, 4:5), 6 statics (4:5, 1:1, 9:16), 1 UGC review (9:16), 10 variations.
- **Launch:** 1 Sales campaign · Ad set A: hook test (4 videos) · Ad set B: visual test (3 videos) · Ad set C: offer test (3 statics) · Advantage+ placements · ₹5,000/day for the first 3 days, then move budget to winners.
- **Risks:** label errors in AI shots → run `product-accuracy-checker` on every asset · "14 days" claim not proven → change to "made for your Diwali glow" · AI model disclosure → caption line + follow current Meta labelling.
