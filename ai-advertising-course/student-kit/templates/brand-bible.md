# Brand Bible

**How to fill:** This one document keeps every image, video and sound in your campaign looking like the
same brand. Fill it in Lecture 2. Make it final in Lecture 9. Attach it to every AI chat (with any skill)
so the AI copies your look every time. The `brand-bible-builder` skill can interview you and fill this.

Copy the **LOOK BLOCK** at the bottom into the end of every image and video prompt.

## Filled example: Kesar & Co.

- **Name:** Kesar & Co.
- **Description:** Indian skincare made with real Kashmiri saffron.
- **Product:** Saffron Glow Face Serum, 30 ml amber glass dropper bottle, gold cap, cream label with a saffron-red wordmark, ₹899.
- **Promise:** Real saffron, real glow.
- **Personality:** warm, confident, premium-Indian.
- **Exact label text:** "KESAR & CO." / "Saffron Glow" / "Face Serum" / "30 ml".
- **AI usually gets wrong:** misspells "KESAR", turns the dropper into a pump, makes the glass clear instead of amber.

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Saffron | #E8862A |
| Secondary | Deep red | #8E1B1B |
| Background | Cream | #F6EBD9 |
| Accent | Gold | #C9A24B |

- **Light:** warm golden-hour, soft side light, gentle highlights on glass.
- **Grade:** warm, clean, slightly rich, no heavy filters.
- **Camera:** 100mm macro for product, 85mm for people, shallow depth of field.
- **Sets we use:** cream silk, brass diyas, marigold petals, saffron threads, warm stone, jharokha windows.
- **Never:** neon, cold blue light, cluttered backgrounds, fairness-cream cues.
- **People:** "Ananya", 27, warm medium-brown skin, natural texture, minimal makeup, gold jhumkas.
- **Voice:** warm, simple, confident. English with light Hinglish. Never say "fair", "whitening", "flawless".
- **CTA:** "Glow for Diwali. Shop now."
- **Sound:** soft female VO, late 20s, Indian English, calm pace. Music: warm sitar + soft electronic beat, 95 BPM.

**LOOK BLOCK:**
```
Brand look: warm golden-hour light, soft side light, rich warm grade, palette saffron #E8862A, deep red #8E1B1B, cream #F6EBD9, gold #C9A24B, cream silk, brass and marigold accents, 100mm macro feel, shallow depth of field. Clean, premium Indian, consistent with previous images. Avoid: neon, cold blue light, clutter, plastic-looking skin, misspelled label.
```
