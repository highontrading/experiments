## Filled example: Kesar & Co.

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Brand | Kesar & Co. |
| Product | Saffron Glow Face Serum, 30 ml, ₹899 |
| Campaign | "Diwali Glow" |
| Launch | 10 days before Diwali, runs 3 weeks |
| Budget | Example: ₹40,000 production, ₹1,50,000 media |
| Deliverables due | 7 days before launch |

- **Business goal:** 1,200 serum orders during Diwali season.
- **Ad goal:** Tap "Shop now" and buy the serum or the Diwali gift box.
- **KPI:** CPA under ₹450, ROAS above 2.0.
- **Who:** Women 22–38 in metro and tier-2 cities, working or studying, buy skincare online 1–2 times a month.
- **What they want:** A visible glow for Diwali parties and photos, without heavy makeup.
- **What stops them:** "Serums don't suit my skin", "too expensive for 30 ml", "I've tried vitamin C already".
- **Where:** Instagram Reels and Stories first, Feed second.
- **The one thing:** *"Real saffron glow in 14 days, ready before Diwali."*
- **Proof:** Kashmiri saffron + niacinamide, 4.6★ from 2,300 reviews (check the claim before you use it).
- **Tone:** warm, confident, festive. **Look:** golden-hour, brass diyas, cream silk, clean premium Indian.
- **Must feel like:** a premium Indian beauty brand. **Must NOT feel like:** a cheap discount sale.
- **Mandatories:** Bottle label always readable. No "fairness" or "whitening" words. Offer terms on end card.
- **Formats:** 1 x 30s film (9:16), 2 x 15s cut-downs (9:16, 4:5), 6 statics (4:5, 1:1, 9:16), 1 UGC review (9:16), 10 variations.
- **Open questions:** Can we say "14 days"? Is the gift box price fixed? Do we have a real customer review we can quote?
