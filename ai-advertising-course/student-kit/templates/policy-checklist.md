# Ad Policy + Rights Checklist

**How to fill:** Run this on every ad before it goes live. Tick each line. If any line is ❌, fix it first.
The `ad-policy-check` skill can review your ad (image, video, script, caption) against this list.

**Important:** Platform rules and laws change. On your launch date, **check the current Meta Advertising
Standards and Meta's AI labelling / disclosure rules**, and for India the **current ASCI code and
guidelines**. This list helps you check. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Truthful claims
- [ ] Every claim in the ad is true and I can prove it (test report, review count, ingredient list).
- [ ] AI images do not make the product look better than it really is (size, colour, texture, results).
- [ ] No "before/after" result that the real product cannot give.
- [ ] Food looks like the real food the customer will get.
- [ ] No fake numbers ("10,000 happy customers") or fake awards.
- [ ] Words like "best", "No. 1", "clinically proven", "dermatologist-recommended" only with proof.

## 2. Health, beauty and sensitive claims
- [ ] No "cure", "treat", "heal" claims for cosmetics.
- [ ] No "fair", "fairness", "whitening" or skin-tone shaming.
- [ ] No body-shaming or "perfect body" messages.
- [ ] Ads do not suggest people's personal attributes (e.g. "Are you overweight?", "Struggling with acne?" said directly to the viewer, a Meta policy risk).
- [ ] Restricted categories (health, finance, alcohol, dating, weight loss) checked against current Meta rules.

## 3. AI disclosure and people
- [ ] AI-made people are made up, not copies of real people, celebrities or influencers.
- [ ] No real person's face, voice or name used without written permission.
- [ ] AI UGC is not presented as a real customer's honest review. Testimonials are real, or clearly a dramatisation.
- [ ] AI disclosure / "AI info" labelling follows Meta's current rules (required for some ad types, such as social issues, elections and politics).
- [ ] Caption has an AI-character line where needed (e.g. "Ananya is an AI-created brand character").

## 4. Rights and ownership
- [ ] My plan on each AI tool allows commercial use (images, video, voice, music).
- [ ] Music is licensed for ads (Suno paid plan, or another licensed source).
- [ ] Client product photos used with the client's written permission.
- [ ] No other brand's logo, packaging or trademark in the ad.
- [ ] No copyrighted characters, artworks or film scenes copied.
- [ ] Fonts are licensed for commercial use.

## 5. Meta format checks
- [ ] Text and logo inside safe zones (9:16: keep out of top ~14% and bottom ~20%).
- [ ] Landing page matches the ad (same product, same price, same offer).
- [ ] Offer terms (dates, limits) on the end card or landing page.
- [ ] No fake buttons, fake notifications or "play" icons that trick people.
- [ ] Ad copy has no all-caps shouting, excessive punctuation or misleading clickbait.

## 6. India (ASCI) quick checks
- [ ] Influencer / creator content marked as an ad where required (e.g. #ad, "Paid partnership").
- [ ] Claims backed by evidence you can share if asked.
- [ ] Offer and price terms are clear and not hidden.
- [ ] No disparaging competitors by name.
