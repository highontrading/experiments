# Start Here

> **Easiest way in:** open `pages/index.html` in your browser. It has every workflow step by step,
> with copy buttons and a progress tracker.

You get **4 skills** and **4 workflows**. Together they take you from a brief to a live ad.

**Skill** = a file you attach to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. It turns the AI into a specialist
that writes your prompts for you.
**Workflow** = the steps to follow, in order.

## The 4 skills

| Skill | Think of it as | It helps you |
|---|---|---|
| `ad-strategist` | **The Planner** | Turn a brief into an idea, hooks, a script and a storyboard. Plan the launch and read the results. |
| `ad-image-director` | **The Photographer** | Turn one product photo into packshots, lifestyle shots, model shots and Meta statics. |
| `ad-video-director` | **The Film Director** | Turn images into video ads, UGC, voiceovers, music and sound in Google Flow. |
| `vibe-editor` | **The Editor** | Edit your video by describing it: cuts, captions, end card, every size. |

## The 4 workflows

```
01 Plan the ad  →  02 Photo → campaign images  →  03 Images → video ad  →  04 Edit & launch
   (Planner)          (Photographer)                (Film Director)          (Editor + Planner)
```

## How to attach a skill

- **Claude:** go to Settings → Capabilities → Skills and upload the skill's zip. Or simply attach
  the `SKILL.md` file in a chat.
- **ChatGPT:** attach `SKILL.md` in a chat, or upload it as Knowledge in a Custom GPT.
- **Gemini:** attach `SKILL.md` in a chat, or upload it as Knowledge in a Gem.

Then type: **"Follow this skill. My product is …"**, attach your product photos and Brand Bible,
and copy the prompts it gives you into **Google Flow** or **ChatGPT**.

## Before you start
1. Pick your brand (your own, a client's, or a made-up one). You'll use it all course.
2. Take 5 product photos: front, both sides, back and top, on a plain background in daylight.
3. Fill in `templates/brand-bible.md`, or ask `ad-strategist` to fill it in with you.

## Main tools
Google Flow (video: Google Omni Flash 1.2; images: Nano Banana 1.1) · ChatGPT (images: GPT Image 2.6)
· ElevenLabs (voice) · Suno (music) · Meta Ads Manager. Other models work too.
