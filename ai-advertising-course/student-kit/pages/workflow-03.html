Plan the shots

Tool
Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini + ad-video-director
You get
a shot list

Attach the skill, your script, brand bible and images. Ask for a shot list.

Type this
Here is my 15s script and my approved images. Make a 5-shot film for a 9:16 Reel. Give me a shot list and a continuity sheet.
Checkeach shot has one action and one camera move, and lasts 3–8 seconds.

Tool
same chat

one Flow prompt per shot

Now write the Google Flow prompt for every shot. Use Frames to Video with my images as start frames.
Checkevery prompt has a "Consistency" line, so the label stays the same.

Step 3 of 7

Make the clips in Flow

Tool
Google Flow · Google Omni Flash 1.2
You get
2–3 takes per shot

Pick Frames to Video (or whichever mode your Flow shows). Upload the start frame, paste the prompt, and make 2–3 takes. Save them in 03_video/.

Checkwatch each take 3 times: for the feel, for the label, and for errors (hands, faces, flicker).

Step 4 of 7

Fix weak shots

Tool
same chat
You get
a better prompt

Tell the skill what went wrong, in one line.

Type this
Shot 3: the label melted at the end. Give me a fixed prompt.
Checkthe label is readable in your best take of every shot.

Add voice, music and sound

Tool
ElevenLabs + Suno
You get
a voiceover, music bed, jingle tag and 3–5 SFX
Type this
Write my voiceover in Hinglish for this 15s script, plus a Suno music bed, a 4-second sonic logo and 4 sound effects.

Paste the voice prompt into ElevenLabs and the music prompt into Suno. Save everything in 04_audio/.

Checkthe voiceover fits the time when you read it aloud with a timer.

Avoid these

Common mistakes

  • Mistake 1Using Text to Video for product shots. Use Frames to Video from an approved image.
  • Mistake 2Asking for two camera moves in one shot. Pick one; slow moves keep the label sharp.
  • Mistake 3Writing the voiceover too long. Keep to about 2.5 words per second.
