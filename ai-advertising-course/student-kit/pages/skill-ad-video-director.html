Ask it like this

Attach the skill, then type requests like these. Use your own product and goal.

  • Turn this serum packshot into a 6-second slow orbit video for Reels.
  • Make a 30-second Diwali film for Kesar & Co. in 5 shots.
  • Give me a giant-product-in-a-city CGI shot for my drink can.
  • Write a Hinglish UGC review ad with an AI creator holding my product.
  • Create a brand ambassador I can use in every campaign.
  • Write a 15s voiceover in Hindi and English, plus a jingle and a sonic logo.
  • I shot this on my phone. Make it look premium with AI.
  • Which shots should I film for real and which should I generate?
What’s inside

What it can do

You don’t pick a mode. Just ask, and the skill picks the right one.

Mode 1

Still → video clip

Your approved image becomes a 4–8s clip in Google Flow, with one clear camera move.

Mode 2

Multi-shot film

Shot list → keyframes → clips, with a continuity sheet so every shot matches.

Mode 3

CGI & recipes

Giant product in a city, splashes, pours, fabric movement, tech reveals.

Mode 4

AI UGC & brand ambassador

Creator-style reviews, unboxings and GRWM in Hinglish, and one face for every campaign.

Mode 5

Voice, music & SFX

Voiceovers in Hindi, English or Hinglish, a jingle, a sonic logo and sound effects.

Mode 6

Hybrid real shoot + AI

What to film for real, what to generate, and how to blend them.

Good to know

  • Tip 1Start product shots from your approved still (Frames to Video). Text-only video breaks labels.
  • Tip 2One shot = one move = one job. Keep clips 4–8 seconds.
  • Tip 3Make 2–3 takes per shot and pick the best. Tell the skill what went wrong in one line.
