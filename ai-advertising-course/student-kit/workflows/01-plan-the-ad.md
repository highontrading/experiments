# Workflow 01: Plan the Ad
> At the end you have a creative brief, one big idea, 5 hooks, a script, a storyboard and a Brand Bible.

| Time | Skill to attach | Tools | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60–90 min | `ad-strategist` | Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini | 1, 2 |

## You need
- Your product (or a client's), with 3–5 clear photos.
- The brief, even if it's just a WhatsApp message.
- Who buys it and why, in one line.
- Your deliverables, e.g. "6 statics + one 15s Reel".

## Steps

### Step 1 · Attach the skill
**Tool:** Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini · **You get:** an AI that works like an agency strategist
Upload `ad-strategist` as a Skill, or attach `SKILL.md` to a new chat.
**Type this:**
```text
Follow this skill. My brand is Kesar & Co. We sell a Saffron Glow Face Serum (30 ml, ₹899). I need a Diwali campaign.
```
**Check:** the AI asks you a few short questions, not a long list.

### Step 2 · Turn the brief into a creative brief
**Tool:** same chat · **You get:** a one-page brief
Paste the client's message exactly as it is and answer the AI's questions.
**Type this:**
```text
Here's the client's message: "Need Diwali ads, premium feel, for women 25–40 in metros, launch in 2 weeks." Make the creative brief.
```
**Check:** the single-minded message is one idea, with no "and".

### Step 3 · Pick the big idea
**Tool:** same chat · **You get:** 3 big ideas and one recommendation
**Type this:**
```text
Give me 3 big ideas for this brief. Test each one and recommend the best.
```
**Check:** you can picture the big idea as one image (e.g. "The Saffron Hour": a golden drop lit like a diya).

### Step 4 · Write hooks and the script
**Tool:** same chat · **You get:** 5 top hooks and a timed script
**Type this:**
```text
Write 30 hooks, keep the best 5, then write a 15s Reel script in Hinglish using hook #1. Offer: 15% off, Shop now.
```
**Check:** the hook works on mute, and the product shows up by 5 seconds.

### Step 5 · Make the storyboard
**Tool:** same chat · **You get:** a 6-frame storyboard with one image prompt per frame
**Type this:**
```text
Turn this script into a 6-frame storyboard. Write one image prompt per frame for Nano Banana 1.1, 9:16.
```
**Check:** every frame prompt has a PRODUCT LOCK line and a ratio.

### Step 6 · Build the Brand Bible
**Tool:** same chat (attach product photos) · **You get:** palette, light, look blocks, never-do list
**Type this:**
```text
Build the Brand Bible for Kesar & Co. Feeling: warm, premium, Indian. Colours: saffron, deep red, cream, gold.
```
**Check:** it has hex colours and copy-ready LOOK BLOCKS. Save it as `01_brief/brand-bible.md`.

## Done when
- [ ] The creative brief fits on one page.
- [ ] You picked one big idea.
- [ ] You have 5 hooks and one timed script.
- [ ] You have 6 storyboard frame prompts, each with PRODUCT LOCK.
- [ ] `brand-bible.md` is saved in your project folder.

## Common mistakes
- Skipping the brief and jumping straight to images. The ads end up pretty but say nothing.
- A message with two ideas ("glow and anti-ageing"). Pick one.
- Fake claims or numbers in hooks ("No.1 serum"). Only use what you can prove.
