# Recipe Vault: 13 Ad Formats

One recipe for each ad format on the course page. Each recipe gives the goal, what reference you need,
a copy-ready image prompt (Nano Banana 1.1 / GPT Image 2.6), a video prompt (Google Flow, Google Omni
Flash 1.2), sound, edit notes for the `vibe-editor` skill, and the mistakes to avoid.

Replace the words in [brackets]. Paste your LOOK BLOCK (Brand Bible) and CHARACTER BLOCK (ambassador bible)
where shown. Check every product shot with `product-accuracy-checker`.

**Contents:** 1 Campaign film · 2 Product motion · 3 Beauty surreal · 4 Model lookbook · 5 CGI giant product ·
6 UGC try-on haul · 7 UGC GRWM / review · 8 Unboxing · 9 Food & bev pour/splash · 10 Beauty macro texture ·
11 Luxury accessories · 12 Luxury editorial · 13 Festive Diwali

## 1. Campaign film
**Goal:** A 30s brand film with a story (problem → product → feeling), cut for Reels and YouTube.
**Reference needed:** product photos, model kit (`W02`), Brand Bible, 6-frame storyboard (`templates/script-storyboard.md`).
**Workflow:** `W05-multi-shot-film.md`

**Image prompt (start frame, shot 2):**
```
Medium shot of [CHARACTER BLOCK] dropping her office bag on a chair and looking tired into a mirror in her warm-lit apartment, diya lights glowing softly on the shelf behind her, the [product] visible on the dresser. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, label readable. Light: warm lamp from the left, diya bokeh. Camera: 50mm, f/2, eye level. Mood: tired but hopeful, real. Brand look: [LOOK BLOCK]. Format: 9:16. Avoid: lighter skin, plastic skin, a messy room.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Medium shot. [CHARACTER BLOCK] drops her bag, sighs, looks at her reflection, then notices the serum on the dresser and smiles slightly. Camera: static, then a very slow push-in. Light & mood: warm lamp, diya glow, quiet. Sound: bag thud, soft sigh, room tone. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same woman, room and product as the start frame, label readable. Avoid: face changing, fast movement.
```
**Sound:** VO (warm female), Suno brand bed 95 BPM, 3 SFX (whoosh, glass clink, sonic logo).
**Edit notes:** "30s Reel, warm and premium, cut on the beat, cream captions, end on bottle + logo + Shop now." Make a 15s cut-down (hook → product → end card).
**Mistakes:** too many ideas in one film · a different face in every shot · the product appears too late (after 8 s).

## 2. Product motion
**Goal:** A 6–10s packshot in motion: the product looks expensive, label perfect.
**Reference needed:** product photos from 3 angles, hero packshot.
**Workflow:** `W01` → `W04`

**Image prompt:**
```
Hero product shot of the [product] on a round cream travertine plinth. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, same shape, label text, logo, cap and colours, label sharp and readable. Set: warm beige wall with leaf shadows. Light: hard golden window light from the left, rim light on the right edge. Camera: 85mm, f/5.6, slightly low angle. Mood: editorial, calm. Brand look: [LOOK BLOCK]. Format: 9:16. Avoid: misspelled label, clutter.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Medium close-up product shot. The [product] stands on the plinth while leaf shadows move slowly across the wall. Camera: smooth 45-degree orbit left to right at product height. Light & mood: golden window light, premium calm. Sound: soft whoosh, light ambience. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same product, set and light as the start frame, label sharp and readable, product does not change shape. Avoid: fast spin, label warping.
```
**Sound:** luxury minimal bed + one glass clink.
**Edit notes:** "8s Reel loop, slow and premium, product name text at 2 s, end card at 6 s, loops cleanly."
**Mistakes:** fast camera moves (labels melt) · a busy set · no rim light (glass looks flat).

## 3. Beauty surreal
**Goal:** A scroll-stopping surreal visual that still sells the product (beauty and skincare).
**Reference needed:** product photos, ingredient (saffron threads, flower, fruit).

**Image prompt:**
```
Surreal beauty scene: the [product] standing on a giant floating saffron flower in a soft cream sky, saffron threads drifting like ribbons, small golden serum droplets floating in the air. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, label facing camera and readable. Light: soft glowing light from behind the flower, warm. Camera: 85mm, f/4, eye level. Mood: dreamy, magical, premium. Brand look: [LOOK BLOCK]. Format: 9:16. Avoid: cartoon look, ribbons covering the label, extra bottles.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Medium shot. The giant saffron flower slowly opens its petals around the [product], saffron threads swirl gently upward. Camera: very slow push-in. Light & mood: glowing, dreamy, warm. Sound: soft shimmering pad, airy whoosh. Duration: 6 seconds, slow. Consistency: same product, flower and sky as the start frame, label readable. Avoid: the product changing, chaotic motion.
```
**Sound:** ambient pad + shimmer, soft VO line "Real saffron. Real glow."
**Edit notes:** "10s Reel, dreamy and slow, one line of text, soft dissolve to the end card."
**Mistakes:** surreal but no product focus · the ingredient is unclear · too dark.

## 4. Model lookbook
**Goal:** 6–10 consistent looks for a fashion catalogue or lookbook (same model, exact garments).
**Reference needed:** garment photos (front, back, detail), model kit (`W02`).

**Image prompt:**
```
Full-body fashion lookbook shot of [CHARACTER BLOCK] wearing the [Loom Street indigo block-print cotton kurta with white palazzos and tan kolhapuri sandals]. GARMENT LOCK: keep the exact print, colour, neckline and length from the garment reference image. Set: Jaipur haveli courtyard with pink sandstone arches. Light: soft open shade, warm bounce. Camera: 50mm, f/4, full body, eye level. Mood: relaxed, confident. Format: 4:5. Avoid: changing the print, a different face, warped hands.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Full-body shot. [CHARACTER BLOCK] in the kurta walks towards the camera, turns at the arch and looks back over her shoulder. Camera: slow tracking backwards with her. Light & mood: soft shade, warm walls. Sound: footsteps on stone, birds, soft music. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same woman and same kurta print as the start frame. Avoid: print melting, face changing.
```
**Sound:** festive or minimal bed depending on the collection.
**Edit notes:** "15s lookbook Reel, cut every 1.5 s on the beat, look name + price in small cream text, end on the collection name."
**Mistakes:** the garment print changes · a different model in every look · stiff poses.

## 5. CGI giant product
**Goal:** A giant product in a real place: impossible, epic, believable.
**Reference needed:** product photos, a real city or location idea.

**Image prompt:**
```
A giant [product], as tall as a 5-storey building, standing in a real Jaipur street at golden hour, auto-rickshaws and people small at its base looking up. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, label readable at this scale. Light: golden hour, long shadows, the product casts a real shadow on the street. Camera: 24mm, low angle from street level. Mood: epic, surprising, believable. Brand look: [LOOK BLOCK]. Format: 9:16. Avoid: toy look, floating product, wrong scale, misspelled label.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Wide shot. The giant [product] stands in the street, traffic moves past its base, a few people take photos. Camera: slow crane up from street level to the gold cap. Light & mood: golden hour, epic. Sound: city traffic, horns, a deep cinematic boom at the top. Duration: 7 seconds. Consistency: same giant product as the start frame, label readable, real shadow. Avoid: scale changing, the product floating.
```
**Sound:** city ambience → boom → brand bed.
**Edit notes:** "10s Reel, start on a street-level close shot, reveal the scale at 2 s with the boom, text 'Bigger glow this Diwali', end card."
**Mistakes:** no shadow (looks pasted) · the scale changes mid-shot · the label becomes unreadable.

## 6. UGC try-on haul
**Goal:** A creator-style try-on that feels native to Reels.
**Reference needed:** garment photos, creator kit (`W02`), the creator's script (`ugc-ad-director`).
**Workflow:** `W06-ugc-pipeline.md`

**Image prompt:**
```
Phone selfie in a bedroom mirror: [CHARACTER BLOCK] wearing the [Loom Street mustard printed kurta set], holding a phone, happy expression. GARMENT LOCK: exact print and colour from the garment reference image. Set: real bedroom, clothes on the bed, a ring light off to the side. Light: natural window light. Camera: phone camera look, slight grain, mirror selfie. Mood: casual, excited. Format: 9:16. Avoid: studio look, perfect posing, a changed print.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Handheld mirror selfie video. [CHARACTER BLOCK] turns left and right in the kurta set and says: "Okay this one is my favourite... look at the print!" Camera: handheld phone, natural shakes. Light & mood: window light, casual and fun. Sound: her voice, room echo, light lo-fi beat. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same woman and same garment as the start frame, lips in sync. Avoid: studio light, the print changing.
```
**Sound:** her voice + light lo-fi bed.
**Edit notes:** "25s UGC haul, jump cuts, outfit number + price as white text with a black outline, fast transitions on the beat, end on 'Link in bio'."
**Mistakes:** looks like a studio ad · too long per outfit · not marked as AI where needed.

## 7. UGC GRWM / review
**Goal:** A get-ready-with-me or honest-review video that builds trust and converts.
**Reference needed:** product photos, creator kit, script with a hook.
**Workflow:** `W06-ugc-pipeline.md`

**Image prompt:**
```
Phone front-camera selfie of [CHARACTER BLOCK] sitting at her bathroom mirror in the morning, hair tied up, holding the [product] near her face. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, label readable. Light: bathroom light + window light. Camera: phone front camera, slight wide-angle, slight grain. Mood: casual, honest. Format: 9:16. Avoid: heavy makeup, beauty filter, studio light.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Handheld selfie video. [CHARACTER BLOCK] holds up the serum and says: "Honestly? I didn't think saffron would do anything. Day fourteen... see this glow?" She tilts her cheek to the light. Camera: handheld, close, natural movement. Light & mood: soft morning light, real. Sound: her voice, bathroom echo. Duration: 7 seconds. Consistency: same woman and product as the start frame, lips in sync. Avoid: perfect stable camera, a changed face.
```
**Sound:** voice only, or voice + very quiet lo-fi.
**Edit notes:** "20s UGC review, hook text in the first 1 s, word-by-word captions, one product close-up B-roll, end on product in hand + CTA."
**Mistakes:** fake "real customer" claims · results the product can't give · script sounds like an ad.

## 8. Unboxing
**Goal:** The first-look moment every D2C brand needs.
**Reference needed:** product photos + packaging/box photos.

**Image prompt:**
```
POV top-down: two hands holding a closed cream Kesar & Co. gift box with a gold foil pattern on a light-wood desk, a small plant and a coffee cup nearby. PRODUCT LOCK: exact box design from the reference image. Light: soft daylight. Camera: POV, 28mm, top-down 60°. Mood: excited, real. Format: 9:16. Avoid: extra hands, other brands, misspelled logo.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
POV handheld unboxing. Hands open the cream box, peel back tissue paper and lift the [product] towards the camera, turning it so the label faces the lens. Camera: POV handheld, natural. Light & mood: soft daylight, satisfying. Sound: box opening, paper crinkle, a happy "ooh". Duration: 7 seconds. Consistency: same box and product as the start frame, label readable at the end. Avoid: the product appearing from nowhere, extra fingers.
```
**Sound:** ASMR sounds loud, music very low.
**Edit notes:** "15s unboxing Reel, ASMR sounds up front, captions 'What's inside the Diwali box', slow-mo on the product reveal, end card."
**Mistakes:** packaging doesn't match the real box · reveal is too slow · no clear CTA.

## 9. Food & beverage pour/splash
**Goal:** Pours, splashes and colour that make people thirsty or hungry.
**Reference needed:** product/can photos; real food reference if you show food.

**Image prompt:**
```
The [Fizzbar sparkling mango drink, slim 300 ml can] bursting through a wave of mango juice, droplets and mango slices frozen in mid-air. PRODUCT LOCK: exact can from the reference image, same label and logo, readable. Set: bright saffron-yellow gradient. Light: hard backlight for glowing droplets, soft front fill. Camera: 85mm, f/8. Mood: fresh, loud, thirst-making. Format: 9:16. Avoid: a deformed can, brown juice, a misspelled logo.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Slow-motion product shot. The [Fizzbar can] rises through a wave of mango juice, droplets flying past the camera, then the can settles and the label faces the camera. Camera: slight slow push-in. Light & mood: bright backlight, energetic. Sound: big splash, can fizz, percussion hit. Duration: 5 seconds, slow motion. Consistency: same can as the start frame, logo readable at the end. Avoid: the can melting, muddy colours.
```
**Sound:** splash + fizz SFX, energetic 120 BPM bed, 2-note sonic logo.
**Edit notes:** "10s Reel, high energy, cut on every beat, speed ramp into the splash, bold yellow captions, end card with price."
**Mistakes:** food looks better than the real product (misleading) · liquid physics look fake · the logo is unreadable after the splash.

## 10. Beauty macro texture
**Goal:** Macro texture shots for glosses, lipsticks, creams and serums.
**Reference needed:** product photo, real swatch photo (so the colour/texture is true).

**Image prompt:**
```
Extreme macro of a single golden serum drop falling from a glass dropper, a tiny saffron thread inside the drop. PRODUCT LOCK: dropper and cap match the reference product. Set: soft cream background. Light: backlight that makes the drop glow, one small sparkle highlight. Camera: 100mm macro, f/5.6, very shallow focus. Mood: luxurious, precise. Format: 9:16. Avoid: an oily look, a plastic dropper, too many drops.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Extreme close-up. A single golden drop slowly forms at the dropper tip, stretches and falls into a small pool of serum, making a gentle ripple. Camera: static macro, then a tiny push-in. Light & mood: warm backlight, glowing. Sound: soft drip, gentle shimmer. Duration: 5 seconds, slow motion. Consistency: same dropper and set as the start frame. Avoid: liquid flowing upward, a wrong texture.
```
**Sound:** ASMR drip + soft pad.
**Edit notes:** "8s Reel, ASMR, slow, one word on screen 'Three drops.', end card."
**Mistakes:** texture/colour different from the real product · unreal "flawless" skin · results claims.

## 11. Luxury accessories
**Goal:** Bags, jewellery and watches in editorial settings.
**Reference needed:** product photos from all angles + close-ups of hardware, stones or dial.

**Image prompt:**
```
A [gold kundan necklace with green enamel and pearl drops] laid on folded deep-red raw silk. PRODUCT LOCK: exact piece from the reference image, same stones, pearls and layout. Light: one soft spotlight from above-left making the gold glow, deep shadows. Camera: 100mm macro, f/8, 45° angle. Mood: rich, heritage, quiet luxury. Format: 4:5. Avoid: extra stones, a warped chain, fake sparkle stars.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Close-up. The spotlight slowly moves across the necklace, each stone catching the light one by one. Camera: very slow slide right, macro. Light & mood: dark, rich, glowing gold. Sound: soft shimmer, deep warm pad. Duration: 6 seconds, slow. Consistency: same necklace and silk as the start frame, stones don't change. Avoid: the necklace moving by itself, extra pearls.
```
**Sound:** luxury minimal bed, soft shimmer SFX.
**Edit notes:** "12s Reel, slow, dark, elegant serif text with the piece name, long dissolve to the end card."
**Mistakes:** the AI adds stones or changes the dial · cheap sparkle effects · too bright.

## 12. Luxury editorial
**Goal:** Minimal, high-fashion product stories for premium brands.
**Reference needed:** product photos, mood references (architecture, art, fashion editorials).

**Image prompt:**
```
Minimal editorial: the [product] placed alone on a white marble step in an empty modern building, a single beam of hard sunlight cutting diagonally across it, strong geometric shadows. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, label readable. Camera: 35mm, f/8, low angle, lots of negative space. Mood: silent, high-fashion, confident. Brand look: [LOOK BLOCK], muted. Format: 4:5. Avoid: props, people, clutter, warm-cosy vibe.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Wide shot. The sunbeam slowly moves across the marble step until it lights up the [product] completely. Camera: locked-off static. Light & mood: hard sun, quiet, minimal. Sound: near silence, a soft low tone, distant echo. Duration: 7 seconds, very slow. Consistency: same product and space as the start frame, label readable. Avoid: camera movement, extra objects.
```
**Sound:** near silence + one low tone; minimal piano for the end card.
**Edit notes:** "15s Reel, very slow, 3 shots only, small thin text, lots of space, end card with logo only."
**Mistakes:** too many shots · loud music · busy text.

## 13. Festive Diwali
**Goal:** A festive campaign set (statics + video) that feels warm, premium and Indian, not cheap-sale.
**Reference needed:** product photos, Brand Bible, offer details.

**Image prompt:**
```
The [product] on cream silk surrounded by lit brass diyas, marigold petals and a small rangoli in saffron and deep red. PRODUCT LOCK: exact product from the reference image, label readable. Light: warm diya glow from below and sides, soft golden key light from the left, string-light bokeh behind. Camera: 85mm, f/2.8, slightly low. Mood: festive, warm, premium. Brand look: [LOOK BLOCK]. Format: 4:5. Avoid: too many diyas, fire touching the product, cheap glitter, misspelled label.
```
**Video prompt:**
```
Close-up. A hand lights the last diya in a row beside the [product]; as each flame catches, the bottle glows warmer, then marigold petals fall softly. Camera: slow slide right along the diyas, ending on the product. Light & mood: warm, glowing, festive. Sound: match strike, flame flutter, distant fireworks, soft bells. Duration: 6 seconds. Consistency: same product, set and light as the start frame, label readable. Avoid: fire touching the product, flickering exposure.
```
**Sound:** festive upbeat bed (dhol, shehnai) or the brand jingle; fireworks ambience.
**Edit notes:** "15s Reel, warm and festive, cut on the beat, gold-cream captions, offer end card 'Diwali Glow · ₹899 · Free gift box'."
**Mistakes:** looks like a discount flyer · too much glitter and red · forgetting offer terms and dates.
