VIBE EDITOR
===========

inbox/       Put your raw videos here.
output/      Finished reels, covers and captions appear here.
brand/       Your logo + brand.json (colours, fonts, caption style, CTA).
broll/       Extra clips or images to cut away to.
music/       Background music you have the rights to use.
references/  Reels or screenshots you love, so Claude can match the style.
fonts/       Your own fonts (.ttf / .otf) — usable in every effect.
sounds/      Your own sound effects (.wav / .mp3) — use them by file name.
looks/       Your own colour grades (.cube LUT files).
styles/      Your saved caption styles, looks and custom effects.

Then just tell Claude:  /vibe-editor:edit  make my latest video a reel
