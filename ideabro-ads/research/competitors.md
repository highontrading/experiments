# Competitor creative scan: AI-skills course ads on Meta

*For: Ideabro Studio, "AI & Advertising" ($29). Scanned 13 Nov 2026.*

**Method.** 17 Meta Ad Library searches of active ads. Most were in India (IN) and the rest in the US, GB and AE. Terms: AI course, AI advertising course, AI ads course, AI video course, AI marketing course, generative AI course, AI UGC, AI product photography, AI influencer workshop, AI tools workshop, prompt engineering course, Midjourney course, AI filmmaking course, AI video ads workshop, AI seekho, AI se paise, AI tools sikhe. About 400 ads came back and about 120 were relevant. Two web searches filled in format context.

**Data limits.**
- The Ad Library tool returns only the **headline (link title)**, the page and the snapshot URL. It does not return the body copy or the media type.
- So the hooks quoted below are headlines. The format split in section 2 is an **estimate**: it comes from cues in the headlines, from repeated variant patterns and from public write-ups.
- Before scripting, open the snapshot URLs to confirm the formats.
- Broad terms in the US and AE return mostly short-drama apps, which are noise. India is where most of the category's ads run.

## 2. Recurring creative formats (estimated share of relevant ads)

- **Talking-head founder or instructor, selfie or desk style: ~35%.** This is the default for workshops (Be10x, the AI-influencer workshops, creator-led pages). The hook is spoken straight to camera and on-screen captions repeat the price.
- **Screen recording or tool demo with voiceover: ~20%.** The demo shows a prompt typed and then the result. Common for AI agents, ChatGPT and faceless-YouTube offers.
- **Before/after or output reel: ~15%.** A montage of AI images and videos ("No Studio. A Whole New Look.", jewellery photo upgrades, AI 3D wedding invites, AI horror content).
- **Static or motion-graphic offer card: ~15%.** Price, date and "Limited seats" in large type, usually in Diwali, Black Friday or Cyber Monday colours.
- **Listicle or carousel: ~8%.** Examples: "14 AI Agents", "22 Professional Courses in One Bundle", "6 Content Bundles for ₹49".
- **Testimonial or case study: ~5%.** Example: "10X Agentic AI Workshop – Case Study Style".
- **Meme or skit: rare, under 3%.**

One sign of iteration volume: the top advertisers run 10–20 near-identical variants of the same headline. Content Creator.com, AIF Workshop and Be10x all do this.

## 3. Caption and text styles

- **Emoji as bullet or opener.** 🔥 🚀 🚨 🤖 💚 🎉 lead about 40% of the headlines.
- **Urgency.** "🚨Only 10 Seats Left", "🔥 Limited Seats Available!", fixed dates ("16–17 Nov", "Nov 23 Batch"), "Before It's Too Late".
- **Price in the headline.** "Sirf ₹99", "Just ₹9", "@ Rs9/-", "Register at ₹0", "Only $7.99", "₹2,999".
- **Free-first.** "FREE AI Marketing Workshop", "free AI course", "100% Free Training!", "FREE webinar", "JOIN FOR FREE TELEGRAM CHANNEL".
- **Speed and ease claims.** "in 30 Minutes", "In Hours, NOT Weeks", "in Minutes", "No Coding Required".
- **Seasonal skins.** "Diwali Dhamaka Offer", "Diwali Mega Bundle", Black Friday and Cyber Monday tags appended to evergreen headlines.
- **Capitalisation for shouting.** "MILLION VIEW", "NOT Weeks", "THE COMPLETE AI HORROR CONTENT MASTERCLASS".
- **Premium exceptions.** Oxford Saïd and ELVTR use calm sentence case, proof points ("96% Student Satisfaction Rate") and outcome framing ("Create Portfolio-Ready Projects with AI").

## 4. Offers and CTAs

- **Tripwire workshops.** ₹0, ₹9, ₹99, ₹199 for a live 2–3-hour session, followed by an upsell. Examples: Be10x, Claraverse "AI Influencer Live Workshop – Rs 199", WorkChain "₹199".
- **Bundles and price anchors.** "Get ALL … Courses for the Price of One", "91% Off + Bonuses", "90% OFF", "50% OFF", "22 courses in one bundle".
- **Bonuses.** Templates, prompt packs ("AI Image Prompt Playbook"), eBooks/PDFs, and Telegram or community access.
- **Guarantees and credentials.** "07 Days Refund Policy", certificates, "100% Job Support", IBM/university branding.
- **Deadlines.** Fixed batch or workshop dates, seat caps, festival or Black Friday end dates.
- **CTA buttons and lines seen.** "Register Now", "Enroll Now", "Start Now", "Chat with us" (WhatsApp/Messenger), "Try it now ----->", "Install Now / FREE Trial", "Join for Just ₹X".

## 5. Gaps a premium, design-led brand could own

1. **Show the craft, not the instructor.** Almost nobody opens on a finished, art-directed output: a campaign-film frame, a luxury product shoot, a model shoot. Most ads open on a face or a price card. Make the work the hook.
2. **Taste as the selling point.** Every competitor sells speed ("in 30 minutes") or money. Nobody sells *quality*: "AI ads that don't look like AI ads." This is open ground for a design studio.
3. **Ads that show the ad being made.** Very few "this ad was made with what you'll learn" reveals, such as a breakdown from prompt to frame to final Meta ad. Each ad can be its own proof.
4. **Calm, premium copy in India.** The Indian market is saturated with emoji, CAPS and ₹9 offers. Restrained typography and confident sentence case will stand out. The global premium players (Oxford, ELVTR) don't target this audience or this skill.
5. **Hinglish that isn't a hustle pitch.** Hinglish ads today are almost all "paise kamao" or fear of job loss. A witty, design-literate Hinglish voice aimed at D2C founders ("Photoshoot ka budget? Zero. Quality? Campaign-level.") is unclaimed.
6. **Speaking to D2C founders by name.** Few ads name D2C founders, marketers or designers in the first second. Most speak to students or side-hustlers.
7. **A transparent, single global price.** $29 one-time, with no tripwire and no upsell funnel, is itself a point of difference against the ₹9-then-₹30k model.
8. **Comparing AI against the real shoot.** Very few run "₹2 lakh photoshoot vs ₹0 AI shoot" comparisons for product, model and UGC side by side. Only tools like Vosu AI hint at it.
