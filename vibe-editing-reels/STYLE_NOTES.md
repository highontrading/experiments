# @earn.edits style notes

Source: reel https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ4dXpRu9g6/ (posted 2026-06-22,
8.7K likes, 64 comments). Seen so far: the cover frame and the caption. The
motion, pacing and audio are still unseen; this file gets updated when the MP4

## What the reel is

A 1930 World Cup history story, told as documentary motion graphics. The post
sells the After Effects project file behind it. Hashtags: #earnedits
#aftereffects #retroedit #worldcup #video_editing.

## Frame anatomy (cover, 720x1280)

Back to front:

1. **Background:** saturated sky photo, `#0F76CD` to `#1686DF`, with soft clouds.
2. **Hero stat:** a huge red **13** (`#F42829`), heavy rounded geometric sans
   (Archivo Black / Futura Extra Bold class), centred, about 60% of the frame width.
3. **Cut-out object:** a white airliner PNG flying *through* the number. It sits
   in front of "13" and gets an orange engine flare (`#F49534`).
4. **Label word:** **TEAMS** in white, the same heavy sans, with a thin dark navy
   outline. A foreground cloud layer covers its bottom third.
5. **Foreground clouds:** a separate layer, which gives a depth sandwich and parallax.
6. **Small stock elements:** a flock of birds and dust specks.
7. **Subtitle:** "traveled to Uruguay", white Helvetica-style medium on a
   translucent grey rounded box (about 60% opacity), sitting under the stat.
   It reads like native IG/TikTok captions.
8. **Finish over everything:** a halftone dot screen across the whole frame,
   film grain, dust and scratch specks, and slight edge darkening. This is the
   "retro" in #retroedit.

## Design rules to carry over

- **One idea per frame:** a big number + one label word + one subtitle line.
- **Layer sandwich:** background, then type, then the cut-out object passing
  through, then foreground elements that cover part of the type.
- **Palette:** primary blue sky, signal red for numbers, white for words, and
  one warm accent.
- **Two type voices:** a heavy display face for stats and labels, plus a plain
  sans in a caption box for the narration line.
- **Print texture** (halftone, grain, specks) on every frame, so photos, PNGs
  and type look like they belong together.
- **Caption copy:** short storytelling lines, one emoji each, a pitch for the
  file, then hashtags. This post has no "comment X" CTA.
