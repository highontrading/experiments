# Candidates (3D in code)

1. CAROUSEL (seed s3: an airport baggage carousel; LEGO-like bricks; the 1920s). 3 a.m., an empty terminal. The baggage belt turns; the screens above it play adverts made for passengers who are not there, each one rewritten for a name on a tag. The camera rides the belt through the rubber flaps into the back hall, where a machine builds the adverts from the same few bricks, thousands a minute. The bags that come out have no owners; phones left on the belt (shopping agents) watch the adverts and buy. Thesis: when a machine writes the advert and a machine reads it, who is it for? Key images: rubber belt slats under sodium light, a wall of screens reflected in a wet floor, a phone on the belt lighting up "Order placed". Form: one continuous shot on the belt. Medium: 3D realistic (lit materials).
2. JOCKEY (seed s1: camel racing; cardboard; a frozen lake; two lives that never meet). In camel racing, the child jockeys were replaced by remote-controlled robot jockeys the size of a shoebox; the camels kept running. A cardboard world: robot jockeys whip cardboard camels round a frozen lake while owners steer them from SUVs by radio. Thesis: the machine can sit on top and steer, but it cannot make anything want to run. Form: a race in one lap, documentary interview fragments as on-screen type. Medium: 3D cardboard (corrugated board, tape, staples).
3. SALT (seed s2: a salt flat at dawn; a match-cut chain; Wes Anderson pink). A plotter rover carves adverts into a salt flat, one after another to the horizon, each a little different, optimised for the satellite above; at night the flat floods and the water erases them all; at dawn the flat is a mirror again. Key images: hexagonal salt crust, a rover's light, a sky doubled in water. Form: match-cut chain from carving to carving. Medium: 3D realistic.
4. SHELF (no seed). A supermarket shelf at night: every product's packaging redesigns itself under the camera, again and again, until all of them look the same. Medium: 3D product render.
5. ECHO (no seed). A concert hall of speakers; an advert jingle is generated, remixed by machines in every seat, until the hall plays only noise; one music box plays the tune plain. Medium: 3D.
