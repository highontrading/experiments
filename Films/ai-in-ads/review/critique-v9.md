# Critique: draft-v9

1. **58.5–62.5 s · The ending never finishes its gesture.** At 58.5 s the arm swings down. From 59.5 s to the last frame the stamp hangs just to the right of the refrain sign, not over it, and the picture holds still for 3 s (the 59.5, 60.5 and 61.5 s frames are the same). The stamp is never lowered, and there is no fade, so the film stops on a frozen mid-gesture. A viewer cannot tell whether the machine stamped the refrain or spared it. **Fix:** move the stamp's hold point about 10% of frame width to the left so it sits over the 아리랑 sign, hold for 1.5 s (59–60.5), lower and retract the arm over 61–62, and fade out over the last 0.5 s.
2. **45–59.5 s · An empty outlined box sits above the refrain sign for about 14 s.** It looks like a placeholder. At 59.5 s it fills with an English "REFRAIN" tag, which labels what the narration and the sign already said, so it reads as a caption added afterwards. **Fix:** remove the box and the tag. If the word has to stay, keep the box hidden until the tag fades in at 59.5 s.
3. **53–57 s · The machine enters cut by the frame.** Its body is half off the right edge, and its arm and head run out of the top edge at 55–57 s. The film's antagonist arrives cropped. **Fix:** move the machine about 12% of width further into the frame, or scale it down about 15%, so its head and stamp stay inside the window frame.
