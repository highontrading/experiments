# Critique: draft-v2 (62.5 s, 960x540, 24 fps)

## 1. The thesis number flashes and is gone: 7.3-7.5 s
What a viewer sees: the counter climbs 2, 7, 18, 64, 156, 420, 1,341 (sheet 1-7 s), then 1,619 / 2,285 / 3,117 (strip-6s), and **3,600** appears only on the last two or three frames before the hard cut at 7.50 (thumb 7.4 s). The number the whole film rests on is on screen for about 0.1 s and reads as a glitch. Meanwhile "The machine finished before lunch" (4.7 s) plays over a counter at about 100, so the line says "finished" while the picture says "barely started".
Smallest fix: make the counter reach 3,600 by 5.0 s, so it lands on "finished", and hold it still for at least 1.5 s. Either speed up the count by about 2.5 s or move the 7.5 s cut to about 8.5 s, with "versions of one advert" held under it.

## 2. The "winner" advert is a grey wireframe, held for 7.5 s: 37.5-45.0 s
What a viewer sees: on a cold grey paper wall, a frame holding two dark bars, three grey lines and a circle (sheet 38-44 s, thumb 41 s). It looks like a layout placeholder, not a shadow-play advert, and it barely changes across seven seconds (action residual 5.5, mostly a slow drift). After "By the next morning, nobody remembers the winner" (38.4 s), nothing new happens until the cut at 45.0.
Smallest fix: make the winning advert a real, readable shadow image (a product cut-out with a one-word slogan) for about 1.5 s, then let it fade or blur away so that "nobody remembers" plays as an action. Trim the scene to about 5 s (cut at about 43).

## 3. The refrain is never shown, so the ending rests on a label: 20.6 s and 59-62 s
What a viewer sees: in 13-27 s the singers change (hat, hanbok, ladder, modern man with a phone), but nothing visual stays the same between them. "Nobody changed the refrain" (20.6 s) has no picture: there is no shared object or line the viewer can follow. The film then closes on an English sign that reads "REFRAIN" above the woman, with the machine beside her (thumb 61.5 s, strip-59s). That is a caption, not a refrain, so the question the film opens with (what do people keep that the machine cannot make?) is answered with a word.
Smallest fix: give the refrain one recurring visual that every singer carries in 13-27 s, for example the same paper strip with the Hangul line "아리랑 아리랑 아라리요", lit the same way each time. Show it on "Nobody changed the refrain" and let the woman hold or sing it in the last shot instead of the "REFRAIN" sign. The lyric line can also simply replace the word.

Also seen (smaller):
- 13-16 s: the caption "Arirang · Korean folk song" is about 6 px tall at phone width (phone-15s) and is set in a default-looking sans that clashes with the serif counter. Make it about 2x larger and set it in the film's serif.
- Narration: "People needed six hundred years for that many." (9.2 s) is stiff. Natural: "It took people six hundred years to make that many." "By the next morning" (38.4 s) reads better as "By morning".
- 11-12.5 s: an empty window with nothing new happening while "six hundred years" plays.
