# Candidates

1. REFRAIN (seed s2: Arirang). A machine writes one advert 3,600 ways before lunch; it took people 600 years to sing one Korean folk song that many ways, and every singer kept the refrain. AI is a verse machine; what lasts in advertising is the refrain nobody varies. Key images: a candlelit shadow-puppet screen; an articulated automaton puppet stamping paper ad slips; slips streaming like ant trails to lit windows; a manual plate "STEP 5" left blank; the Arirang refrain melody resolving plain at the end. Form: shadow theatre organised as an instruction manual (numbered steps), one rule that breaks once. Medium: shadow puppets, candlelit amber.
2. THE CREASE (seed s1: Ottoman velvet cushion with a fold crease kept for centuries). Every ad a model learns from leaves a crease; the film unfolds a cloth of old ads and the machine's new ad still carries the old fold. Images: brocade crescents carved in linocut, a storyboard drawing itself, a chess club rivalry between a copywriter and a model ending in a duet. Form: storyboard come alive. Medium: linocut.
3. VINTAGE (seed s3: Wachau wine terraces). A machine can bottle a million ads a day; a vintage is one year's weather. Ten things a model wrote this morning, listed on a low-res CRT in cyanotype blue, against terraced vines on a stone rooftop; one bottle is repaired by hand. Form: a list of ten. Medium: low-res CRT.
4. COLONY. Ten thousand ad variants scout like ants; clicks lay pheromone; every colony converges on the same ad; one ant breaks the trail. Images: paper-slip ants in shadow, trails to glowing windows, a single slip walking off. Form: nature documentary parody. Medium: shadow puppets.
5. JINGLE CLOCK. Every hour a machine rewrites a town's ad jingle; the clock tower bell keeps one note. Images: a town square in cut paper, a bell, a radio. Form: one day in hours. Medium: cut paper.
