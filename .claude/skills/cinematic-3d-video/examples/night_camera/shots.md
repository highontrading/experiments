# Night camera - shot plan

**Subject:** a vintage black-paint rangefinder film camera (Poly Haven `Camera_01`, brassed edges,
leather strap loosely coiled) on a dark oiled walnut table. Supporting props: two 35 mm film
cartridges and a lens cap in the foreground, a worn leather notebook soft behind, an architect's
clamp lamp (Poly Haven `desk_lamp_arm_01`, repainted matte black) whose shade glows at top of frame.
**Mood:** quiet, late night, a photographer's desk after a shoot. Premium, still, tactile.
**Time:** ~1 am. **Format:** 9:16, 720x1280 render -> 1080x1920 delivery, 24 fps, 4 s (96 frames).

**Key light:** the desk lamp, behind-left of the hero and ~0.6 m above the table, pointing down at
the camera: a hard, warm (2700 K-ish) cone with a visible falloff edge on the table. It rakes
across the top plate and brassed edges (specular glints) and leaves the front of the lens in
soft falloff. Bulb visible as the brightest practical (shade interior glowing).
**Fill:** almost none - faint warm bounce camera-side (~1:10). **Rim:** cool blue city glow
through the window behind (weak area light in the window), outlining the camera's right edge.
**Background:** back wall with a large window; outside, a night city (procedural lit windows,
deep blue sky) that DOF turns into warm/cool bokeh discs. Wall and room fall to near-black.
**Palette:** amber/tungsten key, deep brown-black shadows, steel-blue city.
**Atmosphere:** dust motes in the lamp cone only.

| Shot | Frames | Lens | f | Move | Focus | Viewer notices |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1-96 (4 s) | 35 mm | 2.5 | Slow eased orbit ~35 deg around the camera (left to right, front-3/4 -> nearer frontal-right), with ~10% push-in and a 3 mm handheld drift; low angle, ~10 cm above the table | Lens front / top plate | Glints sliding across brass edges and the lens glass as the angle changes; city bokeh parallaxing behind; lamp glow top of frame |

**Composition:** hero slightly below centre (middle third), table surface in the lower third, lamp
glow + window bokeh in the top third. Leave the top ~15% and bottom ~20% clear of key detail.
**Branding caveat:** the Camera_01 scan's texture carries real maker/lens lettering - paint it out before client use.
**Sound (not in this pass):** room tone, distant city traffic, faint rain, a soft shutter click at the end.
