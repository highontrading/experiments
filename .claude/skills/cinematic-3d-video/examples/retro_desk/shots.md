# Retro desk - shot plan

**Subject:** generic beige 90s desktop computer + CRT on a walnut desk, screen showing a retro
"Describe your film: cool 3D video" app.
**Mood:** nostalgic, warm, quiet late afternoon. **Palette:** amber/honey key, deep brown shadows,
small cool rim. **Format:** 9:16, 720x1280 render -> 1080x1920 delivery, 24 fps, 7 s (168 frames).

**Key light:** low golden sun (~20 deg) through venetian blinds in the left wall -> diagonal
stripes across computer, desk and back wall. Fill: warm, very low (~1:8). Rim: faint cool from behind.
**Atmosphere:** dust motes in the beams, framed print + bookshelf + cork board soft in the background.

| Shot | Frames | Lens | f | Move | Focus | Viewer notices |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1-96 (4 s) | 45 mm | 2.2 | Slow push-in from 3/4 right to near-frontal, 4 mm handheld | Screen | The light stripes, the era, the screen |
| B | 97-168 (3 s) | 32 mm | 3.2 | Low over the keyboard, slide + tilt up, rack focus keys -> screen | Keys -> screen | Texture of keys, then the hero screen |

**Sound:** room tone with birds and CRT hum, warm pad, whoosh into the cut, keyboard clicks in shot B.
