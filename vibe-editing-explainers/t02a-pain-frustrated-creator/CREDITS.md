# T02A · "This was me, every night. Fighting the timeline.": credits

Everything on screen is real footage or a real photograph. Timelines, waveforms, prompt bar,
sticky note, polaroid frames, tags, titles, the red string and the bat silhouettes are drawn
as part of the design.
