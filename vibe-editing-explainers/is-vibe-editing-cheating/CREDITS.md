# Is vibe editing cheating?: credits

Every image and clip is real footage or a real photographed object. The newspaper layout,
case labels, red tags, stamps, marker strokes, comment bubbles, grids and 3D type are drawn
as part of the design. "The Daily Cut" is a made-up paper name, and the comment handles are
invented examples.
