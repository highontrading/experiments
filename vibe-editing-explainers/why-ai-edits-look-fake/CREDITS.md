# Why most AI edits look fake: credits

Every picture is real footage or a real photographed object. On screen it is pushed into
the red / cream / black palette live (pixelation, ordered dither, palette mapping); the pixel
type, pop-ups, bars, bubbles, metronome, trophy, lights and bell jar are drawn as design.
