# 🎬 Vibe Editor

Your AI reel editor, inside Claude. Drop a raw video, say what you want, get a finished reel.

## Install (2 minutes)

1. Open the **Claude desktop app**.
2. Go to **Customize → Plugins → Add → Upload plugin** and choose `vibe-editor.zip`.
3. Start a new chat (Cowork), share a folder where you want your videos, and type:

   **`/vibe-editor:setup`**

   Claude installs the editor (one time, a few minutes), creates your **Vibe Editor** folder and asks a few questions about your style.

## Make a reel

1. Put your raw video in **Vibe Editor → inbox**.
2. Tell Claude, in your own words:

   > `/vibe-editor:edit` my latest video, energetic, Hinglish captions, big title for the hook, follow CTA at the end

3. Claude cuts the pauses and mistakes, writes captions and shows you a **storyboard**.
4. Give notes like a real editor: *"make the title bigger"*, *"remove the part where I laugh"*, *"more zooms"*.
5. Say **"render it"**. Your reel, cover and post caption appear in **Vibe Editor → output**.

## Commands

| type | what it does |
|---|---|
| `/vibe-editor:setup` | First-time install + your brand style |
| `/vibe-editor:edit` | Edit a video into a reel (also: "edit all videos in my inbox") |
| `/vibe-editor:effects` | See all effects, caption styles, looks and sounds |
| `/vibe-editor:cover` | Make a cover + post caption |
| `/vibe-editor:doctor` | Fix any problem |

You can also just talk normally ("edit my new video"). Claude picks the right one.

## What you need

- A Mac or Windows laptop/desktop (8 GB RAM works; more is faster), ~3 GB free space
- A paid Claude plan (Pro is enough to start)
- Internet for the one-time setup

Everything runs on **your** computer. Your videos are never uploaded anywhere by Vibe Editor.

## What's inside

- Auto-cut pauses, "umm"s and retakes · word-by-word captions in 15 styles
- Hindi, Hinglish (Roman or Devanagari), Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi + 50 more languages
- 20+ motion effects: animated titles, tip cards, myth vs fact, tick-on checklists, money counters, CTA pills, zooms, shake, glitch, flash, b-roll cutaways, logo, progress bar, countdown…
- 12 colour looks · 25 original sound effects · background music with auto-ducking
- Reel cover + post caption with hashtags · 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 exports
- Learns your brand: colours, fonts, caption style, CTA

Fonts are open-source (SIL Open Font License / Apache 2.0) and the sound effects are original, so everything is safe for client work.
