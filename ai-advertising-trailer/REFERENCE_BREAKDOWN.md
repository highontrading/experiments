# Reference breakdown: "Industrial 47" (75.3 s)

**Source:** 1280×720, 24 fps, 1,808 frames, AAC stereo.

**How it was analysed:** all 1,808 frames were read as 1-second contact sheets (24 frames each), with full-resolution pulls wherever text needed reading. Scene-cut detection, beat tracking, onset detection, loudness and spectral profiling were run with librosa. Speech was transcribed with Whisper.

## 1. The big idea
It is a national-pride hype reel that runs **archival past → beat-locked montage → iconic speech → logo**.

- The emotion comes from three things: real archival voices, juxtaposition (B/W archive next to 4K), and a cut grid locked to a 144 BPM track.
- There is almost no motion graphics.

## 3. Visual grammar
- **Cuts:** hard cuts only, on the grid. The grid is 1 beat = 10 frames at 24 fps and 144 BPM, and the montage cuts every 2 beats.
- **Transitions:** besides hard cuts there are only glitch, datamosh and pixelate transitions. They mark the four structural turns: open, strobe-out, into the breakdown, and the logo.
- **Text:**
  - Dialogue subtitles are bold white uppercase geometric sans with a soft shadow and no box, centred at the bottom. Each phrase hard-cuts on and off with the speech.
  - The montage itself has no added text.
- **Watermark:** a small white stencil logo, top-right, at about 70% opacity, on every frame except the end card.
- **Grade:** none is imposed. Every source keeps its native look, aspect ratio and channel bugs, and that "real archive" texture is the style.
- **Motion:** almost all of it comes from inside the clips (launches, throws, drone pushes, a jet's motion blur). The only added moves are punch-ins on the interview.

## 4. Sound grammar
- **Cold open:** archival dialogue over a dark bed, plus glitch SFX.
- **Black hold:** a sub swell with the music cut out.
- **The quote:** sits in near-silence, and the drop hits right after it.
- **Main section:** one continuous 144 BPM track that gets denser every ~12 s. Snare/build fills line up with the burst and double-time cuts.
- **Breakdown:** the music falls away under an iconic speech.
- **Ending:** a sub-boom tail under the logo, fading to silence.
