# Ad batch 2: 20 new ads for "AI & Advertising" (₹2,999)

These 20 ads are standalone. Each one has its own hook, angle, caption style and CTA:
- **15 videos:** 11 English and 4 Hinglish.
- **5 statics:** 4 English and 1 Hinglish, each in both 4:5 and 9:16.

Every file is in `meta-ads-plan/out2/`. None of this has been uploaded to Meta.

## What's consistent across all 20
- **Branding:** the Ideabro Studio IB logo badge at the top of every video, and an end card with the price (₹2,999) and an "Enroll now" button. The end card also shows community.ideabrostudio.com.
- **Captions:** they sit in the lower third, roughly y = 1240–1480 px on 9:16. That's above the Reels UI and clear of faces and products. A soft scrim sits under them so they stay readable without covering the shot.
- **Footage:**
  - Your studio films from the course page
  - Your Drive clips: cheetah, snow leopard, Earth→city, dragon, zeppelin, jellyfish, AI creators and more
  - About 30 premium Mixkit stock clips: studio shoots, night edit suites, Diwali candles, phone scrolling
  - A phone screen-recording of your course page
- **Brand safety:**
  - No real third-party logos.
  - Clips showing Adidas, Honda, Miu Miu, Laneige, Vogue, existing-character lookalikes, or third-party watermarks were left out.
  - The Diesel tile in your site's hero carousel is covered with one of your fictional-brand frames in the screen footage.
- **Audio:**
  - Voiceovers are on **ElevenLabs Eleven v4**, the newest model.
  - SFX are on **Sound Effects v3**.
  - New music comes from **ElevenLabs Music**: festive, trendy, chill, pulse and hype tracks. The rest reuses the launch-trailer score.
  - Everything is mixed to about −14 LUFS, with the music ducked under the voice.
- **Hinglish voiceovers** are spoken from mixed Devanagari/English text, so the pronunciation is natural. The captions show Roman Hinglish.

## Notes before you launch these
- **AI disclosure:**
  - Every ad uses AI-generated people and products, and most say so on screen.
  - India requires disclosure for AI-generated ads.
  - Decide on Meta's AI-info setting **before** you create each creative. It can't be added later.
- **Sample figures:**
  - The "₹1,00,000+" shoot quote (Ads 1 and 18) is labelled as a sample estimate. Change the number if you'd rather quote your own real-world figure.
  - The "₹25,000 / ₹30,000 / ₹20,000 / ₹25,000" line items in Ad 1 are illustrative.
- **Testimonials:**
  - Ads 10 and 19 use the three quotes on your course page, word for word.
  - They're framed as IdeaBro students, which matches the site.
  - Keep written permission from those students on file.
- **Tool names:** Ad 9 lists tool names as text only (no logos). Each tool is labelled with a generic use, like "Veo — video scenes".
- **Testing suggestion:** put these into the existing `AIA | SALES | IN | ADV+ | launch` campaign, 5–6 at a time.
  - **Round 1:** Ads 1, 2, 4, 12, 14 and 18. That's a mix of founder, taste, Hinglish and static.
  - **Next:** kill anything under 0.8% CTR after ₹2,500 spend, then rotate in the next batch.

## To rebuild any ad
```
cd meta-ads-plan/ads2 && python3 e01_shoot_quote.py      # or any eNN_/hNN_ script; statics: python3 statics.py
```
- **Toolkit:** `tools/adkit2.py` (caption engine, end cards, phone scroll, prompt UI).
- **Voiceovers:** `tools/vo_batch.py`. Every script, voice and model is listed at the top of the file. It uses `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` and falls back to `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY_2` when the first key's credits run out.
