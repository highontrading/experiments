## 5. Campaign structure (built for Meta's 2025–26 delivery system)

Meta's retrieval system ("Andromeda") groups visually similar ads together. **Changing a headline doesn't make a new ad.** Variety must come from genuinely different concepts: a different angle, format, person or opening visual **[research]**.

```
1) AIA | SALES | IN | ADV+         ← main scaling engine, 70–75% of spend
   Advantage+ sales campaign · objective Sales · optimise for Purchase
   Advantage+ audience; suggestion only: 21–45, interests "Digital marketing", "Canva", "Shopify", "Adobe"
   Existing-customer budget cap 10% · all placements · 7-day click / 1-day view attribution
   10–15 distinct concepts live (each with 1–3 variations) · add 5–8 new concepts a week

2) AIA | TEST | IN | ABO           ← creative lab, 15–20% of spend
   Manual sales campaign · ad-set budgets · broad targeting (India, 18–55, all genders)
   1 ad set = 1 new concept with 3 hook variations · ₹2,000–3,000 per ad set per day · 4–6 ad sets
   Winners move into campaign 1 as the same post ID, so likes, comments and shares carry over

3) AIA | RETARGET | IN             ← 8–10% of spend
   Landing-page visitors 14 days + checkout starters 7 days + 50% video viewers 30 days + IG engagers 30 days
   Excludes purchasers · objection, proof, deadline and bundle ads (see §7.4)

4) AIA | SALES | T1 | ADV+  (week 3+)   ← 10–15% cap
   US, UK, CA, AU, AE, SG, NZ · $29 · USD page variant · English-only creatives
```

**Rules:**
- **Kill:** turn an ad off at 1.5× break-even spend (≈ ₹2,000) with no checkout started, or at 2× target cost per purchase (≈ ₹1,800) with no purchase. Judge after 48 hours, not 6.
- **Graduate:** 3+ purchases at ≤ ₹1,000 and a hold rate ≥ 6% → duplicate the post ID into campaign 1.
- **Scale:** raise budgets 20–30% every 48 hours while 3-day cost per purchase stays ≤ ₹1,000. If it rises above ₹1,200 for 3 days, step back 20% and add new creatives. **Don't edit live winners.**
- **Fatigue:** 7-day frequency above 2.5–3 on cold traffic, or hook rate down 25% from launch, means it's time to make a new version of that concept: new first 3 seconds, same body.

## 7. Creative strategy: the core of this plan

### 7.1 Principles
1. **The ad is the proof.** Every ad is itself a jaw-dropping AI ad. People should stop because the visual looks like a ₹5-lakh shoot, and *then* learn it was AI.
2. **Show the output in the first 1–2 seconds.** Never open on a logo or a slow intro.
3. **The mix:** about 70% vertical video with sound (9:16, 15–35 seconds), 20% statics (4:5), 10% carousels.
4. **Language:** about 60% English, 40% Hinglish. Test Hindi voiceover versions of the winners; the course teaches Hindi voiceovers, so show it off.
5. **Indian specificity wins:** sarees, kurtas, Indian skin tones, Diwali diyas, chai stalls, Mumbai locals, D2C products (ghee, skincare, jewellery, sneakers, coffee).
6. **Compliance:**
   - No income figures ("earn ₹50k/month").
   - No real brand logos ("Nike-style" is fine; the Nike logo isn't).
   - No "you're about to lose your job".
   - The refund and launch deadline must be real.
   - Use "AI-generated" openly. It *is* the hook.

## 10. Risks and how to handle them
- **Creative fatigue:** the main risk. Keep a 2-week pipeline of ready-made concepts.
- **Price competition with ₹9 workshops:** don't fight on price. Sell the outcome ("shoot-quality ads") and the taste, and use the workshop front end for price-sensitive buyers.
- **Ad rejections:**
  - No income claims or before/after earnings.
  - No real brand logos in demo ads.
  - Keep the account history clean, and keep a backup ad account and page under the business.
- **AI-made people:** Meta may add its own "AI info" label. Embrace it in the copy ("Everything here is AI").
- **Scaling too fast:** never raise a budget more than 30% at once. Watch 3-day rolling cost per purchase, not same-day numbers.
