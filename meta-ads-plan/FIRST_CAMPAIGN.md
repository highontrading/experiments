# First campaign: ₹2,000/day launch for "AI & Advertising" (₹2,999)

This is one campaign with one budget and 4 ads, built from videos you already have. The full long-term plan is in `PLAN.md`; this is the version to start with.

## What to expect at ₹2,000/day [est]
- **Weekly spend:** about ₹14,000 (+18% GST).
- **Expected cost per purchase:** ₹1,100–1,400, so **about 10–13 sales a week** (1–2 a day) once the ads settle.
- **Days 1–3:** Meta is still learning who to show the ads to. Zero or one sale per day is normal. **Don't edit anything during these days.**
- **Break-even:** each sale nets about ₹2,480, so break-even is a cost per purchase of about **₹2,100**. Anything under ₹1,400 is a win worth scaling.
- The goal this week isn't 100 sales. It's to find out **which of the 4 ads sells**, so that's where the next budget goes.

## Daily routine for the first 14 days
| When | Check | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–3 | Nothing | **Don't touch the campaign.** |
| Day 4 | Per ad: outbound CTR, cost per landing-page view, checkouts started | Pause any ad that has spent ₹2,500+ with **no checkout started**, or that has CTR below 0.8%. |
| Day 7 | Campaign cost per purchase over 7 days | **≤ ₹1,400:** raise the budget 20% (₹2,000 → ₹2,400), then 20% more every 3 days while it holds. **₹1,400–2,100:** keep the budget, replace the weakest ad with a new one. **> ₹2,100, or 0 sales after ₹8,000:** check the page (do landing-page views turn into checkouts? the target is 6%+) and swap 2 ads. |
| Every Monday | Which ad has the most purchases | Make 2 new versions of the winner with a new first 3 seconds (new hook text or a different opening shot). Add them, and pause the worst ad. |

**Health targets per ad** (from your Vibe Editing data):
- Outbound CTR above 1.5%
- Cost per landing-page view below ₹25
- Hold rate (ThruPlays ÷ impressions) above 6%
- At least 6% of landing-page views start checkout
