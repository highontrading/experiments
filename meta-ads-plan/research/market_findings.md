**How the ₹9/₹99/free workshop funnel works** (synthesised from the pages above)
1. **[S]** A Meta ad leads to a webinar landing page.
2. **[S]** The page uses an extreme price anchor (₹929 → ₹9; $199 → Free), "bonuses worth ₹10,500" and a dated or midnight deadline, and shows a seat count or countdown timer.
3. **[S]** The live 2–3 hour session (or a 2-day weekend) teaches tools.
4. **[S]** At the end comes a pitch for a paid certification or cohort.
5. **[E]** Follow-up runs by WhatsApp/SMS. The economics depend entirely on the back end: ₹9 does not cover ad cost.
6. **[S]** The trust cost is visible: Trustpilot and review complaints about upselling and refunds. https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/be10x.in ; https://trustedrevie.ws/profile/65de443c954b9

**[E] Implications for Ideabro**
- Ideabro's model is the opposite: a **direct-to-checkout** $29/₹-equivalent course.
- That is easier to run on Meta. It optimises for Purchase rather than Lead, and needs no live sales team.
- It must still convert cold traffic on the page. Use the "no upsell trap, one price, lifetime" framing as a differentiator against the ₹9 webinar fatigue.

### B2. Formats and hooks that win (patterns from the titles above)

**[S] observed patterns, [E] interpretation**
1. **Money as proof.** "How I sold AI UGC ads for $600", "…brands actually pay for", "AI ads that print $45k/month", "I tried selling AI ads to jewellery businesses". This is the top format in Hindi. It shows demand for the freelancing angle, but it is risky in paid ads (see D9).
2. **Spec ad of a famous brand.** "I made a $1M Nike ad with AI", "Nike's AI commercial", "IKEA spec ad", "Lamborghini commercial". Organically these work as before/after or "budget vs result" pieces.
3. **Tool-stack titles.** "X + Y = INSANE AI UGC ads", "Nano Banana Pro + Kling". Freshness of tools drives clicks, but it ages fast.
4. **"Full course / free / no paid tools".** Raj Photo Editing's "FREE AI UGC Ads" (370K) and "Create AI Ads for FREE" (399K). **[E]** Indian audiences over-index on "free / cheapest tool" framing. The course FAQ already promises "free and cheapest options for every step", so use it.
5. **Skeptic / backlash content.** Casterline 4.3M and Pleasant Green 3.7M. **[E]** Viewers are fatigued by "AI slop". That validates the course's "taste is the skill" angle as a differentiator.
6. **"One photo → full campaign"** transformation. "I turned ONE photo into a full AI product shoot", "Turn product photos into video ads". The course page already uses this hero claim.
7. **Agency build challenge.** "I built an AI ad agency in 24 hours".

**[E] Hook bank for this product** (adapted from these patterns; policy-safe wording in D9)
- "This ad cost ₹0 to shoot." Show the final ad, then reveal the phone photo.
- "Same product. ₹40,000 studio shoot vs 20 minutes with AI." Split screen. Use real Indian studio price ranges from E2.
- "Feeds are full of AI ads that look fake. Here's how to make one that looks shot."
- "I made a Diwali / wedding-season campaign for a ₹499 kurta brand without a single shoot."
- "Prompt reveal: the exact lighting prompt behind this ₹50,000-looking jewellery ad."
- "One photo → 20 Meta ad variations." Rapid grid reveal.
- Hindi: "AI se ad kaise banaye jo sach mein shoot jaisa lage."

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **India, conservative** (matches socialmasla actuals) | ₹12–15 | 1.5–1.8% | ₹850–1,000 | **₹85k–1.0L** (+GST ≈ ₹1.0–1.2L) | ≈ ₹26–36L |
| **India, base** (good creative, Reels-heavy, better page) | ₹9–10 | 2.5% | ~₹400 | **≈ ₹40k** (+GST ≈ ₹47k) | ≈ ₹14L |
| **India, strong** (top-decile creative and page) | ₹7 | 4% | ~₹175 | ≈ ₹17.5k | ≈ ₹5L |
| **US, $29** | — | — | $27 (Triple Whale median) to $47+ (Superads) | **$2.7k–4.7k/day** | — |

**[E] What this means**
- At an INR price of about ₹999, the conservative case loses money on the front end (ROAS ~1.0). The base case gives about 2.5× ROAS before GST and gateway fees.
- So 100/day in India is plausible on a ₹40k–60k/day budget, but only if creative and page reach the base case. Plan for ₹85k–1L/day until proven.
- US at $29 is roughly break-even on the front end. Use bumps and upsells and treat it as a secondary geo.
- Learning phase: Meta needs about 50 optimisation events per ad set in 7 days (secondary summaries of Meta's guidance: https://jetfuel.agency/what-is-the-meta-ads-learning-phase-how-to-exit-it-faster-in-2026/). At ₹400 CPA that is about ₹2.9k/day per ad set. 100 sales a day (700 a week) clears this easily if structure is consolidated.
- **AOV lever:**
  - ₹999 course + ₹299 bump (prompt pack / Hindi VO templates) at 25% take, plus a ₹2,499–2,999 upsell (The UGC Lab, or a bundle) at 8%, gives AOV ≈ ₹1,290 (+29%).
  - That moves break-even CPA from ₹999 to about ₹1,290 and makes the conservative case survivable.
  - The pixel must send bump and upsell value in the Purchase event (https://theartofonlinebusiness.com/ltotesting/).

## D. Meta best practices, 2025–2026

### D1. Andromeda (retrieval)
- **[S]** Meta's engineering post (Dec 2, 2024): Andromeda is the ads-retrieval stage. It narrows tens of millions of candidates to a few thousand. Reported results: +6% recall and +8% ads quality on selected segments. It was built to handle the surge in ad volume from Advantage+ and generative-AI creative. Meta also reports +22% ROAS for advertisers newly turning on Advantage+ creative AI-driven targeting, and an estimated +7% conversions from image generation. https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/
- **[S]** Practitioner consensus (Jon Loomer, Oct 2025) on diversification:
  - It means **different visuals, formats (video / static / carousel; 9:16 vs 4:5), personas and pain points, and text angles**.
  - Near-duplicates don't count. The old "max 6 ads per ad set" guidance is gone. More ads is not automatically better.
  - Flexible-format ads take up to 10 images or videos plus 5 primary texts and 5 headlines.
  - https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda-creative-diversification/
- **[S]** Meta publishes **no official number of creatives**. Agency heuristics range from 4–6 to 15–25 distinct concepts per ad set. Andromeda groups visually similar ads, so 15 tweaks of one concept count as one ad. https://adscale.com/blog/how-many-ad-creatives-per-ad-set/ ; https://daumenstopp.de/en/guides/meta-creatives-andromeda.html
- **[E]** Aim for **8–15 genuinely distinct concepts live** in the main sales campaign. Add **3–5 new concepts a week**. Build each concept in 9:16 and 4:5.

### D2. Advantage+ sales campaigns
- **[S]** Advantage+ Shopping was renamed and folded into **Advantage+ sales campaigns**. From Marketing API v25.0 the old ASC creation path is retired, and Advantage+ audience, budget and placements replace it. https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/advantage-campaigns ; https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/06/03/advantage-plus-campaign-experience-for-sales-and-app/
- **[S]** Meta's own guidance:
  - Lowest-cost bidding with no cap.
  - Avoid placement exclusions.
  - Phase spend in (start around 30%, then 70–100%).
  - Combine ad sets, and keep under 20% of spend in learning. Meta claims this cuts cost per purchase by up to 68%.
  - (Facebook Business pages summarised in search results. Not directly fetchable: login wall.)
- **[S]** Meta-reported Advantage+ results: 14% lower CPL for Advantage+ sales, app and lead campaigns (Q3 2025 call). The Advantage+ annual run-rate grew from $20bn (Q4 2024) to $75bn+ (Q2 2026). https://paceads.com/research/meta-ads-statistics-2026
- **[S]** Independent counterpoints:
  - Haus, 640 incrementality tests: Advantage+ Shopping beat manual in only **42%** of tests.
  - Wicked Reports: Advantage+ new-customer CAC rose from $257 to $528 (May 2024 → May 2025).
  - https://www.adexchanger.com/measurement/for-meta-marketers-automation-isnt-always-the-advantage-but-its-complicated/ ; https://www.wickedreports.com/blog/meta-advantage-research-results
  - **[E]** Keep a manual or ABO testing lane as a control.

### D3. Broad targeting
- **[S]** Basic Advantage+ sales campaigns run with only location targeting. Existing customers can be included or excluded with custom audiences.
- **[E]**
  - Go broad in India (18–45, all genders).
  - Let creative do the targeting: separate concepts for D2C founders, freelancers, designers and students.
  - Exclude purchasers.
  - Run a small retargeting budget only after volume builds (site visitors, IG engagers, video viewers).
  - Use the 500+ alumni list as an exclusion or seed audience.

### D4. Conversions API (CAPI) and pixel
- **[S]** Meta Blueprint: pixel + CAPI showed a **13% CPA improvement** on average (28 A/B tests, 2022). A newer figure (17.8% lower cost per result) appears only in secondary reports. https://www.scribd.com/document/706145089/Performance-Fundamentals-Conversions-API ; https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-pixel-conversions-api-ai-updates-2026
- **[E]** Send Purchase from the server with value and currency, event_id dedup, and hashed email and phone. Include bump and upsell value. Indian checkouts collect phone numbers, which improves match quality.

### D5. Value optimisation
- **[E]**
  - Start with Purchase (highest volume) optimisation.
  - Move to "Highest value" or ROAS-goal bidding only after about 50+ purchases a week, once AOV varies (bumps and upsells).
  - At one fixed price, value optimisation adds little.

### D6. Creative testing frameworks
- **[S]** **3-2-2**: dynamic creative with 3 creatives, 2 headlines and 2 primary texts, giving 12 combinations in one ad set, usually run as ABO for 7–14 days. Its originator (Charley Tichenor) has said post-Andromeda that it should be treated as a *minimum*, and that you should test multiple *concepts*. https://www.superads.ai/blog/ad-creative-testing-strategies ; https://segwise.ai/blog/facebook-ad-creative-testing-best-practices.md
- **[S]** Low-ticket rules: test visuals first, then copy, then headlines. Wait 2 days between new variations. A challenger replaces the control after beating it for 2–3 days with positive ROAS. https://theartofonlinebusiness.com/ltotesting/
- **[E] Recommended structure**
  1. One **Advantage+ sales "scale" campaign** (CBO), holding 70–80% of budget, with proven concepts.
  2. One **ABO "test" campaign** (20–30%). Each ad set holds one concept with 3–5 executions. Graduate winners into the scale campaign.
  3. Optionally, a small **retargeting / alumni-proof** campaign.

### D7. Frequency and fatigue
- **[S]** Meta sets no official threshold. Practitioner watch-line for cold prospecting: **2.5–3.0 7-day frequency**, or CTR down 20–30% from baseline over 3–7 days. Refresh every 2–4 weeks. Swapping a headline on the same footage may not reset fatigue, because Andromeda groups by visual similarity. https://brandsearch.co/blog/facebook-ads-frequency-benchmarks-niche-when-to-stop-scaling-2026 ; https://segwise.ai/blog/how-often-refresh-ad-creatives
- **[E]** At about ₹40k–1L/day in India, broad audiences are large. Expect fatigue to come from creative wear-out (2–3 weeks per concept) rather than audience saturation.

### D8. Reels and Stories placements
- **[S]** Reels share of inventory is rising:
  - Over 50% of Instagram ads ran in Reels in 2025 (Sensor Tower, via CNBC).
  - Tinuiti: Reels went from 26% to 35% of IG impressions (Q3 2025 → Q2 2026).
  - https://paceads.com/research/meta-ads-statistics-2026
- **[S]** India CPC: Reels ₹8–18 vs IG Feed ₹14–28 (OwlClaw).
- **[S]** Meta's commonly cited claim (secondary sources): spec-built 9:16 Reels ads with sound show about 34.5% lower cost per result than image ads in the same placement. Vuori A/B test: 17% lower cost per purchase with Reels. https://coinis.com/how-to/design-facebook-reels-ad ; https://business.instagram.com/success/vuori
- **[E]** Make **9:16 video with sound and captions** the default for ~70% of the creative mix, with text inside the safe zones. Use 4:5 statics for Feed.

### D9. Policies: AI disclosure and income claims
**AI disclosure**
- **[S]** Self-disclosure of photorealistic AI media is mandatory only for **social issue / election / political ads**. Meta applies an "AI info" label automatically (three-dot menu). A photorealistic AI human made with Meta's own tools can get a more visible label. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/SIEP-advertising/SIEP/ ; https://www.showfolk.ai/blog/ai-generated-images-facebook-ads-rules
- **[E]** For a course about AI ads, an "AI info" label is harmless. You can even exploit it: "Yes, this ad was made with AI. That's the point."

**Income claims** — Meta Community Standard, Fraud, Scams & Deceptive Practices
- **[S]** It prohibits "**get-rich-quick**" content, defined as "offers of opportunities of unrealistic financial reward for unclear or minimal effort". It also bans jobs with "advance promises of salary" and guaranteed jobs. It names "**celebrity and brand bait**" as a prohibited practice. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/fraud-scams/

**Personal attributes**
- **[S]** Ads must not assert or imply personal attributes, **including "vulnerable financial status"**. Example: "Are you struggling with money?" is not allowed. "You/your" language is fine without an attribute. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/

**Practitioner reports**
- **[S]** Income claims are "the single biggest reason course creator ads get rejected". Examples: "My students make $10K/month", "quit your 9-to-5". Landing pages are reviewed too. https://course-coach.pages.dev/courses/facebook-instagram-ads/13-compliance-and-help/ ; https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-ads-income-claims-policy

**[E] Rules of thumb for this product**

Avoid in ads and on the landing page:
- "Earn ₹50k/month", "make ₹15k per shoot guaranteed", "quit your job", "passive income"
- "Are you a struggling freelancer?"
- Real Nike/Apple logos in "Nike-style" spec ads (brand bait / IP risk)
- "100% placement"

Use instead:
- **Market-price facts and skill framing.** "Brands pay studios ₹5,000–₹10,000+ per hero product image. Learn to create them yourself." Studio prices are sourced in E2.
- Plus "the course includes a module on pricing and pitching AI ad services". State the effort honestly: 41 lessons and a capstone.
- Keep any earnings examples factual, attributed and with disclaimers, on the landing page only.

**Price comparisons**
- **[S]** "Deceptive pricing" is listed among prohibited commercial practices. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/deceptive-content/prohibited-commercial-practices/
- **[E]** Make sure the $99 / ₹ anchor is a real price that has been or will be charged. A permanent "70% off launch" countdown is a review risk.

### D10. PPP and regional pricing
- **[S]** PPP pricing is common for digital products. Typical discounts are 20–40% for lower-income regions, and some course sellers give up to 75% off for India. Vendor case-study lifts (+15–30% revenue) are unverified. Watch for arbitrage or VPN abuse and cross-border GST/VAT. https://checkoutjoy.com/docs/features/products/purchase-power-parity/ ; https://businessbloomer.com/tag/online-courses/ ; https://dodopayments.com/blogs/purchasing-power-parity-pricing-saas
- **[S]** Indian price anchors: Udemy ₹449–609; creator AI courses ₹199–₹1,499 (Claraverse ₹199 workshop, Manik Das ₹499, Marketian ₹1,299, Lapaas ₹1,499); Be10x ₹9.
- **[E] India pricing**
  - $29 converts to about ₹2,400+, which would sit above most India comparables.
  - Test **₹999 vs ₹1,499** (anchor ₹4,999). Keep USD $29 for Tier-1.
  - Consider a $19 PPP tier for other emerging markets.
  - Run **separate campaigns per currency zone**: India INR; US/UK/CA/AU USD; rest of world, optional. Their CPMs differ about 8–12×, and Advantage+ would otherwise pour budget into the cheapest geo.
