WHY MOST AI ADS FLOP

Anyone can press generate.
Few can direct.

Feeds are filling up with AI ads that all look the same. The tools are easy. Taste is the skill. This course teaches the direction that makes AI ads look shot, not generated.

Do I need design or advertising experience?
+

No. We start from the basics of briefs and ideas, then build up to finished photos, videos and Meta ads step by step.

+

You need a laptop and an internet connection. AI tool subscriptions are not included, but we show you the free and cheapest options for every step, and when a paid plan is actually worth it.

Will AI ads look fake?
+

Only when nobody directs them. Most of this course is about art direction, product accuracy and brand consistency, so your ads look shot, not generated.

+

Yes. You learn to make ads for your own products and for clients, plus how to price and pitch AI ad services.

+

Yes. Meta allows AI-generated ads. We cover how and when to label them, and the rules to follow.

The UGC Lab goes deep on one format: AI UGC ads. AI & Advertising covers the whole field: product shoots, model shoots, campaign films, sound, Meta ads and more.
