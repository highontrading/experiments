# Meta Ad Library: competitor findings for "AI & Advertising" (Ideabro Studio)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Method: 25 `ads_library_search` calls (19 keyword, 6 page_id), mostly ACTIVE ads, countries IN, US, GB and AE. The raw rows and snapshot IDs are in `adlibrary_raw.md`.

Labels used below: **[O]** means observed in the Ad Library data. **[I]** means my inference or outside context, not visible in the data.

## 0. What the data can and can't show (read this first)

- **[O]** The API returns only page name, page ID, the **link headline** (`ad_creative_link_title`), start date, currency and snapshot URL. It does **not** return primary text or body copy, CTA button, landing domain, media type, spend or impressions. So every "hook" below is the **headline**, not the first spoken or written line. To see formats (UGC, screen recording, static and so on) and body hooks, you have to open the snapshot links by hand.
- **[O]** Results come back newest first. Big keyword pools only show ads that started in the last 1–3 days, so I couldn't directly find "oldest still-running" ads. Page-ID queries helped. Where a page had fewer than 50 active ads, I saw its full set.
- **[O]** The oldest still-active ads I found in this niche started around **27 Oct to 1 Nov 2026**, about 2–2.5 weeks ago. Examples: Simone Ferretti "🚨 SALE 75% OFF" (30 Oct), Clicktools Pro Diwali (30–1 Nov), Tyler Tometich (31 Oct), Aimble (28 Oct). **This category does not let single ads run for months. It churns creatives in batches.** Be10x has 87 active ads, all started 7–13 Nov. Content Creator.com launched 50+ variants within one hour. Ritika launched 17 variants in the same minute.
- **Winner signal used instead of age [I]:** a headline that is **relaunched again and again in new batches**, or that keeps many variants alive for more than 10 days. Examples: Be10x "Become AI-Proof Before It's Too Late" (relaunched 7, 10, 11, 12 and 13 Nov); Simone "Learn How To Make AI Content In 2 Days." (11 and 13 Nov, 25+ variants); Tyler "Create MILLION VIEW Videos with your PHONE!" (31 Oct to 12 Nov); Ritika "Turn AI Skills Into Income" (20 variants, live since 3 Nov).
- GrowthSchool and Skill Nation did **not** show up through this tool. Keyword search matched generic "school" ads. Be10x and Outskill did show up.

## 4. Offer, price points and funnels: India vs US

**India [O]**
- The entry tier is ultra-low: ₹0 (Outskill), ₹9 (Be10x), ₹99 (BuildOur AI 2-day workshop, prompt/ideas packs), ₹149 (prompt pack), ₹199 (Claraverse live workshop, InstaGizmos, Growwkey), ₹299 (MS Digital), ₹499 (Digital Shakti 9-day mini course).
- The mid tier is ₹969 (9-course bundle), ₹1,500–₹1,999 (10-day Meta ads courses) and ₹2,999 (Diwali offer).
- The high tier is "Enquire Now" lead forms (Nextless, institutes with "100% placement").
- Funnels: (a) free or ₹9–₹199 **live workshop** → upsell (dominant; upsell inferred); (b) direct low-ticket course; (c) **click-to-WhatsApp / Messenger** ("Chat with us", "WhatsApp for a free sample", landing `api.whatsapp.com`); (d) lead form for long programs.
- Language: Hinglish is common (Deepak, Nextless, Zonfly), plus Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu ads (Loopive "Claude AI in Malayalam", Entri, Jhalak).
- **[I]** $29 is about ₹2,400. In India that sits **above** almost every AI entry offer seen (₹9–₹499) and level with the ₹1,500–₹2,999 courses. India needs either an INR price closer to ₹499–₹999, or a ₹99/₹199 front end (mini-workshop, prompt pack or module 1) that leads into the full course. Without one of these, the "70% off" message competes with ₹9 offers.

**US / global [O]**
- Tripwire courses at **$27–$29** ("7 modules and 6 bonuses for $29", "$27" trainings), memberships ($79/mo Hybrid AI Filmmaking), Skool communities, and $1–$8/mo tools.
- **Permanent deep discounts** re-labelled by season: Simone's 75% off (SALE → CHRISTMAS → BLACK-FRIDAY), Tyler's 91% off, Content Creator's Black Friday/Cyber Monday.
- Free live trainings and webinars ("Free + Live – NOV. 17th", "FREE Training at 8 PM EST").
- **[I]** Ideabro's $29 is right at the US tripwire norm. The value-stack framing ("11 modules / 41 lessons / lifetime") matches what works there. Black Friday / Cyber Monday is live right now (13 Nov), and US competitors are already running BF creative.

## 6. Gaps this course could own

1. **"Shoot-quality ads without a shoot", taught as a course.** [O] Only tools and services use the no-shoot angle (Victogrvphy, Vosu, Aivx, MastiLearn, CINQA). No course seen owns it. Ideabro is a studio, so it can show real client-grade outputs.
2. **Taste / art direction.** [O] Only Deepak SIsodia (graphic design) sells "design sense". Nobody sells "AI makes anyone produce images; taste makes them ads". This is a natural fit for a studio brand. Counter-positioning against generic "AI tools" courses (Be10x, Outskill) is open [I].
3. **D2C founders as DIY learners.** [O] D2C founders are heavily targeted by *agencies* ("₹2L → ₹16L/Month", "Scale Your D2C Brand 🚀", "100+ AI UGC creatives built for D2C brands") but not by a "make your own ad creatives" course. Angle [I]: "Your agency charges ₹X per shoot. Make the same ads yourself for ₹Y."
4. **Indian festive and wedding-season campaigns as a skill demo.** [O] Festive themes appear only as discount labels (DigiMark Diwali, Clicktools Diwali). Nobody shows "make your brand's Diwali / wedding-season / Republic Day campaign film with AI". [I] It is wedding season now (Nov–Dec), and Christmas, New Year and Republic Day are coming.
5. **Hindi + English voiceovers and jingles (ElevenLabs/Suno).** [O] Not seen in any competitor headline.
6. **The full campaign workflow (stills → video → UGC → Meta testing → pitching) vs single-skill courses.** [O] Indian competitors sell narrow single-topic offers (AI influencer ₹199–₹299, prompt packs ₹149, generic "AI tools" ₹9). US has narrow ones too (AI content, VFX).
7. **Selling AI ad services, for Indian freelancers and agencies.** [O] US has "Build an AI Agency in 90 Days" and "Pay In Full After This AI Gets Clients". India has only vague "income skill" claims. Module 11 (pricing and pitching) is a strong angle, but needs compliant framing (see section 7).
8. **Self-paced, lifetime access, no webinar pitch.** [I] The Indian market is saturated with live-workshop upsell funnels. "No webinar. No upsell. 41 lessons, lifetime, ₹___" could be a trust differentiator.
9. **Show the output first.** [O] Only small new players (AI VIDS LAB, 4 ads; AI Video Academy, 3 ads) are on this angle. It is low competition and fits a studio that can show a showreel.

## 7. Compliance patterns seen

- **Income and outcome claims [O]:**
  - "Turn AI Skills Into Income" (Ritika)
  - "Your Next Income Skill Starts at ₹199" (Growwkey)
  - "Add $200k-$2m/Year In Profit With AI" (Aretech)
  - "₹2L → ₹16L/Month In 6 Months" (Perfomity D2C)
  - "100% MNC Job Placement" (Vinsup)
  - "Get Packages Up To ₹12 LPA*" (Skill Circle, uses an asterisk)
  - "Earn ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 Monthly" (beauty school)
  - **[I]** Meta's policies on misleading claims and "get rich quick" make specific earnings claims a rejection and account-quality risk. For module 11, say "learn how to price and pitch AI ad services" rather than naming rupee figures.
- **Fear framing [O]:** "Become AI-Proof Before It's Too Late" and "Be on the Right Side of the AI Line" are in use and approved. **[I]** Avoid second-person attribute assertions such as "You're about to lose your job" (Meta's personal-attributes rule).
- **Permanent "X% off" [O]:** Simone's 75% off stays constant while the label rotates (SALE / CHRISTMAS / BLACK-FRIDAY); Tyler shows 91% off. **[I]** Ideabro's "$99 → $29, 70% off" should rest on a genuine reference price and a real end date. India's CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines (2023) cover false urgency and false scarcity, and "only 2 seats" style claims (ITS Gwalior) are risky.
- **AI disclosure [O]:** Not visible through the API, and no headline seen discloses AI-generated people. **[I]** This course's ads will feature AI humans and UGC. Meta may auto-apply "AI info" labels to photoreal AI media. Turning disclosure into the hook ("Everything in this ad is AI. Learn to make it.") gets ahead of the label instead of fighting it.
- **Third-party tool names [O]:** Competitors use tool names freely: "Learn Claude", "with Higgsfield", "More studio for less than Higgsfield Plus". **[I]** Use the names descriptively ("taught with Veo, Kling, Midjourney…") and don't imply partnership or endorsement.
- **"Certified" claims [O]:** "Become A Certified AI Powered Video Editor" (Matchframe). **[I]** Only use this if a real certificate exists.
