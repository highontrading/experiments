## What makes it work

1. **Pace.** Something new every 0.3-1.0 s; no static shot longer than ~1.5 s. The visual idea changes on almost every phrase.
2. **Literal visuals.** Every claim is SHOWN: "superpowers" = hero cutaway, "cool graphics" = new backgrounds, "animations" = MG run,
   "placements" = the frame moving around, "10+ skills" = skill cards, "format" = the frame breaking back to full screen.
3. **Three worlds.** A-roll → screen-in-screen on a laptop (with real hands) → pure MG / white board → back to A-roll. The format
   change itself is the payoff.
4. **Subject-aware effects.** Text behind the head, X-ray skeleton, white-glow and red-fill silhouettes, background replacement
   behind the cut-out. All need a clean matte.
5. **Transitions are 2-3 frames.** Inverted/solarised flashes, pixelate, light flash, silhouette glow, rounded-window reveal.
   Never a slow dissolve.
6. **Typography system.**
   - Wide extended heavy (Archivo Expanded / Monument style), white with a blue gradient: hero words behind the head
     (CLAUDE, RUPEES, THIS VIDEO, INSANE, SUPER POWERS).
   - Tall condensed heavy in RED (Anton / Bebas style): punch words on the chest (THIS SHIT, COOL GRAPHICS, VIDEO FORMAT, 10+ SKILLS).
   - Clean small sans for lead-in words (i have, TO MY, EDITOR) and the CTA ("EDITING" in quotes).
   - Words reveal letter by letter (~1 frame per letter) with blur, often preceded by a white light-flash box.
   - Chrome / metallic 3D for the brand name.
7. **Colour.** Warm tungsten A-roll; accents electric blue and pure red; the MG run goes neon pink / purple / blue; white boards are
   pale blue-white.

## Sound design (measured)

- Continuous music bed: the pauses between words never drop below about -29 dB (the bed sits about 10-14 dB under the voice).
- Hits land on the exact transition frame: 0.41 s (glitch-in), 3.87 s (SUPER POWERS, the strongest transient), 5.78 s (skeleton
  transform), 10.29 s (INSANE), 11.05 s (red flash), 12.46 s (background change), plus dense small ticks / whooshes through the MG
  run (13-15 s) and on each caption reveal.
- Speech sits at about -15 to -18 dB RMS, consistent; no dead air.

## Things we will NOT copy

- The hero cutaway is copyrighted film footage. A Meta ad using it gets flagged or rejected. Cutaways for our ads must be our own
  footage, licensed stock, or AI-generated cinematic b-roll.
- The real hands in the laptop section are physical footage we do not have; we can build the screen-in-screen world as a
  device mockup with handheld motion instead, or the user can film the laptop.
