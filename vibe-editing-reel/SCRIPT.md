# "Vibe Editing": Script and Recording Guide

The structure follows the reference beat for beat: a hook built on a visual paradox, a
reaction, self-describing captions, the reveal of how it's done, technical proof, the pain
of the old way, the twist reveal, then the comment keyword CTA.
**About 108 words, about 35 s.** Read it fast and conversationally, at about 3 words per
second, like you're telling a friend something you can't believe.

## Recording rules (these make or break the edit)

**Camera**
- Shoot **vertical, 4K, 30 fps**. Not 60 and not 24, because the edit is built at 30.
- **Lock exposure and focus** (long-press on iPhone, AE/AF lock), turn **off** beauty filters,
  and use the 1× lens. Use 0.5× only for the overhead shot.
- Put the phone on a tripod. Don't hand-hold except shot 3, and even there a tripod is
  better because you walk toward it.

**Background and wardrobe** (I'll replace every background, so make cutting you out easy)
- Any room works, but **plain wall, nothing behind you that matches your clothes**, and
  **no backlight** (don't put a window behind you).
- Wear a **dark solid hoodie, hood up**, like the reference. A hood gives a clean edge with no
  flyaway hair, so the "text behind my head" effect stays sharp.
- Use **soft light from the front or side** (a window beside you is perfect). Even light on
  the face matters because I'll relight warm.

**Framing**
- **Leave headroom.** In shots 1, 3, 4 and 8, keep **the top 35 % of the frame empty** above
  your head. That's where the text and the orbit live.
- Keep your **whole head in frame at all times**, apart from the duck in shot 2.
- **Overhead (shot 6):** phone about 2.2 m above you, pointing straight down (ladder, top of a
  wardrobe, or a friend standing on a chair). Your whole body should be in frame with
  space around it, because the text runs along your outline.

**Performance**
- Record **each shot as a separate clip**, **3 takes each**. Start each take with **1 second
  of silence**, then speak, then **1 second of silence**.
- Look **into the lens** in shots 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8. Look **away** in shot 1 (side profile).
- Shot 2: really commit to the duck. The comedy of the edit lives there.
- Shot 7: after "exist", **freeze and smile for 2 s**. I use that for the real-room reveal.
- Record **10 s of silence** (room tone) in each location.

**Audio**
- Use a lav or the phone 30–50 cm from your mouth, in a quiet room. Turn off fans and AC.
