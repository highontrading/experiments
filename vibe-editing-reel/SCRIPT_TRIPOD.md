# "Vibe Editing": One Tripod, Standing (one-take version)

You stand in front of one locked tripod and talk. All the camera work is added in the edit:
punch-ins, slow push-ins, whip pans, shake, a new room for every beat, plus the text,
flashes and HUD.

## Setup (2 minutes)
- **Phone:** vertical, **4K, 30 fps**, 1× lens, exposure and focus locked, beauty filters off.
  4K matters because it gives room to crop in up to about 2× without losing quality. That's
  how the close-ups and push-ins get made.
- **Tripod:** at chest height, **about 2.5–3 m away**. Frame yourself **head to knees**, with
  about **35 % empty space above your head**. Don't fill the frame; I'll zoom in later.
- **You:** dark solid hoodie, **hood up**, plain wall behind you, no window behind you, soft
  light from the front or side.
- **Mark two spots on the floor:** **A** (your start, far) and **B** (two steps closer to the
  camera).

## The script (one continuous take, about 35 s)

| # | Do this | Say this | What I add in the edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At **A**, turned **sideways** (profile), looking off to the side | "Wait… who taught these words to hide **behind my head?**" | Slow push-in on the profile, words orbit your head and slide behind it, white-silhouette flash, cream poster with "**head?**" |
| 2 | Snap to **face the camera**, then **duck down fast** out of frame and come back up | "**No way!**" | Camera shake on the duck, "NO WAY" smashes into the wall with cracks and debris |
| 3 | **Walk slowly from A to B** while talking | "And every sentence gets its own vibe… like **neon signs**… **massive bold capitals**… or **soft little serif italics.**" | Wide shot, a whip pan into a tighter crop, neon that relights the room red then blue, stacked capitals, italics that turn to dust |
| 4 | At **B**, stand still and talk with your hands | "It's called **vibe editing**. I tell **Claude Code** the vibe… and it designs every single word… even the ones **behind my head.**" | Cut to a new room, medium punch-in, "Claude Code" in coral, font-spec overlay, orbit words, "**head.**" |
| 5 | Shift your weight and turn slightly to one side, relaxed | "It's tracking my **face thirty times a second**… and it knows exactly where Instagram's **buttons** are… so my captions **never** hide underneath them." | Tight off-centre crop that follows your face, face-tracking box, "**30x a second**", arrows to the button area, dashed safe zone, "**never**" in red |
| 6 | Arms **slightly out from your body**, hold still | "Normally you'd spend hours cutting yourself out **frame by frame**… just for **one word**… but I'm literally **just standing here.**" | Wide full-body shot, words written along a curve around your outline, selection-line outline, background melts into a spotlight |
| 7 | Lean in a little, then after the last word **freeze and smile for 2 seconds** | "Oh, and **this room?** … It doesn't **actually exist.**" | Close-up punch-in, outline "ROOM", wireframe room, then a cut to **your real room** |
| 8 | Make a frame with your hands, then **point down-right** | "Comment **VIBE**… and I'll send you the whole **workflow.**" | Medium shot, gold "**VIBE**", arrow toward the comments, "**workflow.**" |

**Bold words are the payoff words.** Hit them a little harder; they get the big type.

## Teleprompter version
> Wait… who taught these words to hide behind my head? No way! And every sentence gets its

> you the whole workflow.

## Tips
- **Pause half a second between numbered lines** (the "…" marks). Every pause is a cut point.
- If you fluff a line, **pause, then repeat just that line** and keep going. I'll use the best
  version.
- Do the **whole thing 3 times**, then record **10 seconds of silence** standing still. That
  gives me a clean background and room tone.
- Keep your **whole head inside the frame** the entire time, except during the duck.
