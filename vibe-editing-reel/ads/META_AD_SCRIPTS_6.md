# Vibe Editing: 6 Meta Ad Scripts

All six are original scripts. The reference ad only inspired the structure:
**hook → proof → pain → mechanism → offer → button CTA**.
Each ad attacks a **different angle**, so you can run them as separate ad sets and see which
audience bites.
`[BRACKETS]` = fill in with **true** facts (price, lessons, deadline, URL, real time taken).

**Every ad ends on the Meta button.** Not "comment" (paid ads have no DM automation by default).
**No other brands' logos**: write the words, struck through.
**Length:** 30–45 s. The first 2 s must work with the sound off, so the hook is always on screen as text too.

## Ad 1: "The Same-Video Problem" (angle: differentiation)
**For:** creators already using AI tools. **Length:** ~40 s

| # | Say | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Tumhari AI wali video 3 second mein pakdi jaati hai."** | A-roll. Chrome **"3 SEC"** behind your head. Text: *AI edit = pakdi gayi* |
| 2 | "Wahi yellow captions. Wahi zoom-in. Wahi template. Log scroll kar dete hain." | 3 generic reels in a grid, each stamped **SKIP** |
| 3 | "Ab ye dekho." | White flash, then your v2 reel at full screen |
| 4 | "Words mere sir ke peeche ghoom rahe hain. Room asli nahi hai. Har caption alag design hai." | Reel moments, each labelled with a small chip: *depth*, *AI room*, *custom type* |
| 5 | "Aur ye sab maine kisi editing app mein nahi banaya." | ~~CapCut~~ ~~Premiere~~ ~~After Effects~~ |
| 6 | "Main Claude ko bas vibe batata hoon. Wo poora edit code se likh deta hai." | Terminal: prompt typing, then a render bar |
| 7 | "Isko kehte hain Vibe Editing. Aur ye template nahi hai — har video unique banti hai." | Chrome **VIBE EDITING** behind your head |
| 8 | **CTA:** "Agar tumhari videos bhi sabse alag dikhni chahiye, neeche button pe tap karo aur Vibe Editing seekho." | Lime pill **"Learn Vibe Editing →"** + URL |

**Alt hooks:** "Ye video kisi editor ne nahi banayi." / "AI edits boring kyun lagti hain? Ek reason hai."

## How to plan the 6 ads

| Ad | Angle | Best audience | Funnel stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Raw vs Vibe | Proof | Broad / cold | Top |
| 1 Same-Video | Differentiation | AI-tool users | Top |
| 4 Never Edited | Beginner | Aspiring creators | Top / mid |
| 5 One Sentence | Curiosity | Tech / AI fans | Mid |
| 3 ₹ Math | Money | Coaches, brands | Mid / bottom |
| 6 Early-Mover | Career | Editors, students | Mid / bottom |

**Start:** launch Ads 2, 1 and 4 first, each with 2 hook variants (6 creatives). After 3–4 days, keep
the 2 with the lowest cost per landing-page view. Then add Ads 3, 5 and 6 as retargeting for
people who watched 50 %+ of the first wave.

**Recording:** the same setup for all six (warm lamp desk, 4K 30 fps vertical, lav, 30 % headroom).
Record all A-roll lines in one sitting, changing your shirt between ads so they don't look like
one video.
