# Vibe Editing: Meta Ad Scripts

These are original scripts built on the reference's skeleton:
**call-out → proof → second pain → mechanism + offer → button CTA**.
Everything in `[BRACKETS]` is yours to fill in with **true** facts: price, lesson count, bonuses,
deadline, URL.

**Record (A-roll only, the edit adds everything else):**
- 4K, 30 fps, vertical, phone on a tripod at eye level, seated at your desk.
- Warm lamp behind you, off to one side. The look you want is warm and moody, not a white wall.
- Lav mic. Leave **30 % headroom** above your head for the chrome text that sits behind you.
- Say each numbered line as its own take, with a 1 s pause between lines.
- Record **all five hook variants** (below) for every script, so Meta can test them.

The **B-roll / inserts** column is built in the edit from what you already have: `out/vibe_editing_reel_v2.mp4`,
`out/vibe_editing_comparison_ad_v2.mp4`, a screen recording of Claude Code, and your course page.

## Edit spec (premium, full screen, no laptop)

- **Canvas:** 1080×1920, 30 fps. Keep captions and buttons inside the central 4:5 zone and clear of
  the Reels UI (top 14 %, bottom 35 %, 6 % on each side).
- **A-roll:** warm grade, chrome 3D words behind your head on beats 1 and 8, slow push-ins,
  white flash on every cut into an insert.
- **Proof section** (lines 3–7): change the look about every 1.2 s, alternating your reel at full
  screen, the blue/indigo "AI world" cards with frosted chips and glowing lines, and the dark UI
  cards.
- **Offer section:** black + lime UI, counters, before/after split.
- **CTA:** a lime pill button with a gentle pulse, your URL in small mono type underneath, and
  the face cam staying visible.
- **Sound:** an ElevenLabs music bed ducked under your voice, a whoosh on every flash cut, one
  impact on "Ab ye dekho", and a shimmer on the button. Keep it sparse.
- **Exports:** one 9:16 master per hook variant, plus a 4:5 crop for Feed placements.
