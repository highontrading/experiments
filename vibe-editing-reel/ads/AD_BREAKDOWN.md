# Reference Ad: Frame-by-Frame Breakdown ("Claude ne edit ki hai")

**Source:** 720×720 (1:1), **30 fps**, **47.2 s**, **1,414 frames**, in Hinglish.
**Method:** I viewed every frame on contact sheets of 60 frames each, and found the cuts with
ffmpeg scene detection. The speech was transcribed with Whisper large-v3 (Hindi) and then
translated. Frame numbers are 0-based; divide by 30 to get seconds.

---

## 1. Why it sells (the persuasion skeleton)

| Beat | Time | Job | What he says (translated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Call-out + bold claim** | 0.0–8.7 | Stop the scroll and pick a fight | "Everyone who edits with Claude, I can tell with my eyes closed. Their videos are boring and all look the same. Watch this video." |
| **2. Proof (the ad is the demo)** | 8.7–21.4 | Show the result before explaining anything | "This whole video was edited by AI. Each and every second. I didn't edit a single second of it. No CapCut, no Edits, no After Effects, nothing. And now Claude edits your videos like this." |
| **3. Second pain** | 21.4–30.1 | Add a cost they already feel | "Every second here is Claude's edit. And look at this: how many credits does your boring editing burn? Mine get saved." |
| **4. Mechanism + offer** | 30.1–38.0 | Name the secret, show the product | "Because I gave my Claude these **4 skills** and **250 animation skills**. I tell it what I want and it builds it from these." |
| **5. CTA + scarcity** | 38.0–47.1 | Ask, then add urgency | "Want the link to all these premium skills? Comment, I'll send it right now, or take it from the bio. And this won't be for everyone, because I don't want my videos to look like everyone's. So grab it fast." |

The core moves:
1. **The enemy is "everyone else's AI edits."** He isn't selling AI; he's selling *not looking like
   other AI users*.
2. **The video proves itself.** Half the ad (8.7–21.4 s) is the AI-edited video. The viewer judges
   quality with their own eyes before any pitch.
3. **The mechanism is concrete and countable:** "4 skills + 250 animation skills". Numbers make the
   course feel tangible.
4. **The scarcity is identity-based** ("I don't want my videos to look like everyone's"), which
   flatters the buyer.

## 3. Visual system

- **A-roll:** seated at a desk, three-quarter angle, a warm practical lamp behind him (tungsten,
  creamy highlights), dark jacket, handheld lav mic visible. The camera is static; the only moves
  are punch-ins.
- **Chrome 3D type behind the head:** "CLAUDE", "AI", "SECOND", "NAHI". Liquid metal, all caps,
  wide grotesk. This is the same depth trick as the first reference reel.
- **Two graphic worlds:**
  - Electric **blue/indigo gradient** (#2A2AE6 → #0D0B6E) with soft glowing orbs, frosted white
    UI chips and glowing connector lines. This is "the AI world".
  - **Near-black UI** (#0B0B0C) with a **lime accent** (#C8F03A) for product and pricing pages.
- **Kinetic captions:** small white sans, highlighted words in **rounded colour pills** (cyan,
  pink, orange), and serif italic for the emotional word (*"Claude did."*, *"edit"*).
- **Transitions:** white flash frames, blue light-burst with chromatic aberration, and a 3D page
  tilt.
- **Sound:** handheld lav voice, loud and present. The audio never drops below about −27 dB, so
  there is likely a low bed under the voice.

## 4. What to change for **your** ad (no laptop)

He films his laptop with his phone. That reads as authentic, but it looks low-res and cheap in a
paid feed. For a premium full-screen version:

1. **Every "laptop" moment becomes a full-frame insert.** Play your edited reel full screen, or
   picture-in-picture over a blurred background, instead of filming a screen.
2. **Keep the A-roll real.** The talking head is what builds trust; the graphics only prove the
   point.
3. **Use text, not other brands' logos.** Showing CapCut, After Effects or Claude logos in a paid ad
   risks trademark rejection, and can imply endorsement. Struck-through words do the same job.
4. **Paid ads need a button CTA, not "comment".** On a Meta ad, "comment and I'll DM you" has
   no automation behind it unless you set one up. Point to the **Learn More / Sign Up button and
   your URL**, and keep "comment VIBE" for organic posts.
5. **Scarcity must be true.** "Price goes up on [date]" or "[N] seats" is fine; "not for everyone"
   is fine as positioning. Don't invent a deadline.
6. **Export 9:16 (1080×1920) with the key action inside the central 4:5 area**, so the same file
   works in Reels, Stories and Feed.
