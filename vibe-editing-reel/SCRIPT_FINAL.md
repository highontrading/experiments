# "Vibe Editing": Final Script (teacher's version, one tripod, standing)

This is an original script. It borrows only the reference's structure: a hook, a reaction,
captions that act out what you say, how it works, proof, the old way, the twist, the CTA.
The message: **vibe editing is a workflow, and the video they're watching is the proof.**
About 111 words, about 37 s.

Setup is the same as `SCRIPT_TRIPOD.md`: 4K 30 fps, tripod 2.5–3 m away, head to knees,
35 % headroom, hood up, floor marks **A** (far) and **B** (two steps closer).

| # | Do this | Say this | On screen (edit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At **A**, turned **sideways**, looking off | "Look closely… these captions are moving **behind me.**" | Words orbit your head and sink behind it. Flash to the cream poster with "**behind me.**" behind your hood. |
| 2 | Snap to camera, **duck fast** out of frame, come back up | "And I didn't animate **any of it!**" | "ANY OF IT!" smashes into the wall with cracks and debris. |
| 3 | **Walk slowly from A to B** | "This is **vibe editing**. No keyframes… you just describe the vibe. Make it **glow**… make it **BIG AND BOLD**… or **soft and elegant.**" | "glow" is neon and relights the room. "BIG AND BOLD" is stacked capitals. "soft and elegant." is a serif italic that dissolves. |
| 4 | At **B**, talk with your hands | "Then **Claude** writes the code that builds the edit… cuts, captions, motion… even the words **behind my head.**" | "Claude" in coral with the spark, a font-spec overlay, orbit words, "**head.**" |
| 5 | Turn slightly sideways, relaxed | "It tracks my **face every frame**… knows where Instagram's **buttons** sit… so captions **never** get covered." | Face-tracking box and numbers, "**30x a second**", arrows to the button area, dashed safe zone, "**never**" in red. |
| 6 | Arms slightly out, hold still | "Normally that's **hours** of masking and keyframing… for **one reel**. I just **stood here and talked.**" | Text running along your outline, selection-line outline, background melts into a spotlight. |
| 7 | Lean in; after the last word **freeze and smile 2 s** | "Oh, and **this room?** … It isn't **real either.**" | Outline "ROOM", wireframe room, cut to your real room. |
| 8 | Hands make a frame, then **point down-right** | "I teach this whole workflow. Comment **VIBE**… and I'll send you the **details.**" | Gold "**VIBE**" with a light sweep, arrow toward the comments, "**details.**" |

## Teleprompter
> Look closely… these captions are moving behind me. And I didn't animate any of it! This is
> vibe editing. No keyframes… you just describe the vibe. Make it glow… make it big and
> bold… or soft and elegant. Then Claude writes the code that builds the edit… cuts,
> captions, motion… even the words behind my head. It tracks my face every frame… knows
> where Instagram's buttons sit… so captions never get covered. Normally that's hours of
> masking and keyframing… for one reel. I just stood here and talked. Oh, and this room?
> It isn't real either. I teach this whole workflow. Comment VIBE… and I'll send you the
> details.

Bold words get the big text, so stress them. Pause about half a second at each "…".
Do 3 full takes, then record 10 s standing still in silence.
